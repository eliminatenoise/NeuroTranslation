# Sources and permissions

Billur Barshan and Kerem Altun supplied *Daily and Sports Activities* (2010), UCI Machine Learning Repository, DOI 10.24432/C5C59F. UCI distributes it under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. The study selected two activities, four participants and the accelerometer axes of five units, grouped rows into histories and targets, then fitted forecasts and computed descriptive errors. Any excerpt included here retains the dataset's CC BY 4.0 terms. [Dataset](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/256/daily) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

The programme's general learner and its aligned-forecast code are included with the benchmark. The right-arm reference checkpoint and its implementation come from the neighbouring benchmark in this repository. NumPy and PyTorch are external requirements.
