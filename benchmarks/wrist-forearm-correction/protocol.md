# Can forearm EMG improve a wrist forecast trained on movement?

This is an exploratory study within the six previously designated training people. Its new evidence will be six person-held-out folds defined before fitting. The earlier published three-person scores motivated the question. Those people, the six reserved participants and all remaining participants are outside this study. No previously fitted weights, normalization, individual predictions or demo data enter it. No release before separate source-bound review.

## Models and nested separation

For outer person h, only the other five supply fits, scales and negative-control donors. Split before any estimation. All axes and models use exactly the same event IDs. Each trained temporal convolution uses the published temporal study's fixed model, seed 17, batch 128, 30 full epochs, deterministic CPU float32, participant-balanced sampling with replacement, AdamW learning rate .001 and decay .0001. Both joint and motion networks start from scratch with zero predicted change, the same schedule, topology except input channel count, and no early stop. A single seed replaces the historical three-member ensemble for this comparison. Standardize channels over fit people and all bins, and anchors and target-minus-anchor over fit rows, with population SD and unit fallback for zero scale. No scaler is estimated on an evaluated person.

* Fit a fresh motion-only CNN (2 channels) on each of six possible five-person subsets, and a direct joint motion+EMG CNN (6 channels) on each same subset. Fit the six-channel model jointly on the final target from initialization. The frozen five-person motion model is the base at outer inference.
* To learn corrections to *out-of-person* base errors, fit a fresh motion-only CNN for each of the 15 unique four-person subsets. In each outer fold, predict the fifth fit person with the model and scalers that exclude that person and h. A four-person model can serve both admissible outer folds without refitting. Consider these 15 distinct inner models, six outer bases and six outer joint models the complete network inventory.
* Learn two added corrections to those five out-of-person residuals. The response is measured target minus its inner motion prediction, in degrees. One ridge sees 32 flattened anchor-relative angle bins, two last absolute angles and six time flags. The other sees exactly those features, plus 64 flattened EMG amplitudes. Standardize each continuous feature position using the five fit people only (population SD, replace exact zeros by one). No scaling of flags or labels. Fit once per outer fold and correction arm in float64 with equal total weight per person, objective weighted mean squared error plus 0.01 times sum of squared continuous slopes, six unpenalized offset intercepts and no separate intercept. Add the predicted correction to the frozen five-person base. Outer held predictions never provide fitting labels or normalization. In parallel report persistence and constant-velocity extrapolation using the current angle and the actual angle 64 ticks earlier.
* Negative control: keep held motion, time, anchor and target fixed. Substitute a complete 16-bin EMG window from the outer fold's five-person fitting bank. For each held trial with zero-based source ordinal j use its same block and offset. Cycle donor people by j modulo the number with that block; within the selected donor's sorted eligible trial list use index (j+1) modulo its length. This also fixes missing-block handling. Report donor IDs privately and verify every donor is in the five, differs from the held person and matches block and offset. A cross-person substitution changes person as well as trial. A secondary same-person control reverses the order of only the 16 EMG bins within the actual held row, using no other held trial and no future sample. Score both control inputs through the fitted EMG correction and the joint network without refitting.

## Outcomes, qualification and decision

For every participant and axis report mean squared error, RMSE and signed mean error. Overall RMSE in degrees is the square root of the unweighted mean of six per-person, per-axis MSE pairs. Also give paired participant contrasts, leave-one-person-out contrast ranges, axis-wise errors, pooled RMSE and errors by cue offset. Primary contrast is EMG correction minus matched motion-only correction, with negative favoring EMG. Secondary contrasts compare corrected models with base, aligned with wrong-trial/reversed control, direct joint with base, and corrected with direct joint. A competent motion base must improve overall on both persistence and constant velocity. State explicitly if it fails. A candidate forearm benefit requires the direction of the primary contrast and the comparison with the raw base to favor the aligned correction. Report the controls and person sensitivity even if inconsistent. No significance, generalization, natural-language, real-time or causal claim. Preserve failures and unfavorable folds without retuning. These six reused training people support method development only.
