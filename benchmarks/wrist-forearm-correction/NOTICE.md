# Sources and rights

Jorge German Perez Blanco and Javier Mauricio Anteliz Ortiz published the EEG-EMG-WristMotion Dataset, version 3, under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. NeuroTranslation verified the paired recordings and derived wrist-angle averages and four forearm voltage amplitudes. It used those features to fit the models and calculate the aggregate forecasts and errors. The summary data retain their source attribution and CC BY 4.0 terms where applicable. The original recordings remain at Figshare. [Version 3 release](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.29666735.v3) · [Data descriptor](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-026-07287-z) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

The project dedicates its original code, documentation and invented example to CC0. The source parser, temporal convolution model and forearm feature calculations derive from NeuroTranslation’s earlier wrist studies. NumPy and PyTorch are separately installed dependencies with their own licenses.
