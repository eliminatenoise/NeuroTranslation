# Learning a correction to a force forecast

Measure whether adding force records from another lab improves a shared predictor of signed vertical force impulse over the next 100 milliseconds. Each predictor receives ten 20-millisecond mean force readings from one plate. The target sums the next 100 native 1 kHz force readings in newtons and multiplies by the sampling interval of 0.001 seconds. Both fitting packets store signed upward-positive force. Use that sign for histories and targets in both archives. The five native force and watch engineering activities corroborate positive Force_Z for loading. Keep values near zero with their original sign.

The first fit uses only the original twelve-person overground walking fitting archive. The mixed fit adds the six fitting people from the native force and waist-watch study. Use only the plate recordings. In each source give equal weight to people, then to their recordings, then to the eligible rows in each recording. Give the two sources equal weight in the mixed fit. No waist-watch readings or muscle recordings enter this experiment.

Compare the last completed mean held constant and a line through the last two bin centres. Fit both a ridge regression and a single-layer 24-state gated recurrent network to correct the held-constant forecast, using the first source alone and the mixture. A fifth learned method uses the same mixed recurrent network and directly predicts the future impulse. Force is scaled by 1000 N and impulse by 100 N·s in all learned methods. Fix the ridge penalty at 0.001 on slopes only. For each recurrent arm use identical initial weights and sampling seeds 104, 211 and 389, 360 updates of 96 windows, AdamW at 0.002 with weight decay 0.001, smooth L1 loss with transition 0.03 and gradient norm limit 2.0. Initialize the output to zero. Keep all seeds and score both their mean prediction and each individual seed. The recurrent weights, inputs and training use FP32 on CPU without autocast. The ridge fit and scoring use FP64.

Issue all forecasts and seal the learned weights before reading outcome arrays for this run. Score the separate fixed-speed treadmill archive from eight people and the three people already scored outside training in the native force and watch study. Krumm's treadmill recordings come from a laboratory absent from both fitting populations. The second is seen only by the mixed model, through different people. Both evaluation archives have been scored in earlier studies. This comparison is a retrospective check. The reserved original final tests stay closed.

Score mean absolute error in newton-seconds on identical valid rows across methods. Average within recording, then across recordings for each person, giving each person equal weight. Retain individual scores for verification and publish aggregates by evaluation archive. Report paired resampling by person with 4,000 draws using seed 3469. On the treadmill archive, reproduce the earlier three-pass full Research Edition and physical trend scores from the retained forecasts and outcomes. Keep the frozen full Research Edition separately.
