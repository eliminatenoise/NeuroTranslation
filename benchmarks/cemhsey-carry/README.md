# Reusing a pinch correction on another day

How long does one practice pinch buy you? The earlier study fitted a correction on the first of two attempts, then checked it on the second. Here we bring that correction back on the next indexed recording day. The forearm electrodes and the gauge measure a new session. The target on the screen follows the same schedule.

At each of three force targets, the earlier correction faces two alternatives. We can fit it again from the new day’s first pinch, or use the scheduled cue without a personal adjustment. All eleven forecasts face the same second pinch. We compare them first at 50% of that day’s maximum voluntary contraction.
