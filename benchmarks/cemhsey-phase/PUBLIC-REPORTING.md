# Reporting the range for three people

The protocol specifies paired gains for three evaluation participants and records their mean, minimum and maximum. For three values, the mean and the two extremes recover the middle value. The numerical study keeps all three statistics at the specified precision for independent checking.

This public reporting rule was fixed after the fitting stage and before any evaluation recording was opened. Public tables and plots retain the group mean and the counts of people with lower, equal or higher error. They report an enclosing range on a grid of 0.0005 in fractions of maximum voluntary contraction, equivalent to 0.05 percentage points of MVC. The range describes these three people. It is not a confidence interval.

Start with the independently checked minimum and maximum, each rounded to nine decimals. Subtract 0.0000000005 from the minimum and round down to the next grid boundary. Add 0.0000000005 to the maximum and round up to the next grid boundary. When all three unrounded differences are exactly zero, both bounds are zero. The allowance covers rounding in the numerical summary. The new column names identify lower and upper bounds. The public export omits the exact minimum and maximum.
