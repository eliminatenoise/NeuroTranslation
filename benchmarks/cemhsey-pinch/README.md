# Forecasting a two-finger pinch

In CEMHSEY GRASP a participant follows a cue while pinching one dynamometer between thumb and index finger. The force history and cue are strong baselines. The prespecified comparison asks what two short windows of forearm surface EMG can add to a forecast 24 force ticks ahead. The recorded scale is normalized to the participant’s maximum for that day and grasp.
