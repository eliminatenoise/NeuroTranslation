# Locked 300 ms change forecasting on reserved participants

The target is signed change in projected point spread. For each tissue, subtract the spread at the newest labelled ultrasound sample in the last pre-issue 20 ms slot from the spread at the observed target. The target times fell 291.7–308.3 ms after issue. The input is ten past bins of position, muscle voltage where applicable and availability flags. The earlier browser preview estimates absolute spread.

The original six reserved recordings were retrieved after the five methods and six recurrent checkpoints were frozen. Versioned range checks and per-member ZIP checks verified capture. Archive preparation sealed outcome arrays apart from input histories. The issuer read only inputs and frozen weights. Scoring joined the saved estimates to the measured outcomes by participant, issue tick and time.

The six people supply 7,503 common observations per tissue. The table reports mean absolute error in millimetres, averaging participant errors equally within each tissue. Each recurrent forecast averages two independently fitted seeds.
