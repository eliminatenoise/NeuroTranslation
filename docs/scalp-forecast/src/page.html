Can early scalp signals forecast arm movement?

A cue tells a person how to move. Give the model the cue and a quarter-second head start. What do scalp electrodes add to what the arm sensors already tell it?

Measurements and limits

What did the electrodes pick up?

Electrical activity around the eyes can appear at scalp electrodes. A scalp signal that varies with an eye channel raises a different question from one that improves a forecast. Correlation describes how two recordings vary together. Prediction asks whether adding a recording reduces error on later movement in people outside the fit.

These forecasts describe three instrument readings during instructed upper-limb movements. Their anatomical scale and sign remain unresolved. Scalp potentials alone cannot locate the generator of a signal.
