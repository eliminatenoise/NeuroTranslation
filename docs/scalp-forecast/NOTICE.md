Patrick Ofner, Andreas Schwarz, Joana Pereira and Gernot R. Müller-Putz at Graz University of Technology published *Upper limb movements can be decoded from the time-domain of low-frequency EEG* in 2017. They released the source recordings and channel codebook under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International and request citation of the paper. [Recordings and codebook](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.834976) · [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0182578)

NeuroTranslation selected motor-execution trials, summarized early scalp, eye and movement readings, fitted prediction models, and calculated their errors on later movement. Model coefficients and aggregate outcomes derived from the recordings retain attribution under CC BY 4.0. The starting example consists of invented values. The electrode drawing shows the six named scalp sites schematically. The placement of an eye icon beside the scalp drawing does not assign a location to any numbered eye channel.
