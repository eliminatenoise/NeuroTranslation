Prepare earlier wrist frames

The reader takes one movement at a time. It needs the camera’s earlier positions for the wrists, elbows and shoulders. You can run it from the extracted folder with the network disconnected.

Start from a working input

Open the wrist reader, choose a fictional movement, expand “Run your own earlier frames” and save the fictional input. Replace the six landmark histories and frame indices with your own. JSON numbers use a decimal point.

  • domain must be cross_person_wrist.
  • implant_side and annotated_wrist establish the two roles. Use L with r_wrist, or R with l_wrist. For a fictional or bench test, these fields specify a side convention. For a recording, verify the actual side assignment.
  • frame_indices lists 15 consecutive nonnegative integer camera indices in chronological order. The study uses 30 frames per second. If a reach begins at time t seconds, the first frame at or after onset is j = ceil(30t). Supply frames j − 15 through j − 1. The forecasts are for j + 7 and j + 14.
  • prior_by_name contains R_Wrist, R_Elbow, R_Shoulder, L_Wrist, L_Elbow and L_Shoulder. Each track holds 15 pairs, each ordered [image x, image y] in detector pixels. Use null for a missing coordinate. You may also omit an entire missing track.
  • To run a wrong-partner comparison, add earlier_donor with its own frame_indices and prior_by_name. Use the most recent eligible earlier event in the same recording and partition. Its final prior frame must be at least 900 frames earlier, in a different 120-second block. The reader checks the indices. The recording identity and choice of donor are yours to verify.

The input contains earlier frames only. The private-input view shows forecasts without error scores. The fictional examples supply separately generated later positions to illustrate scoring.

What counts as missing?

Missing shoulder pairs prevent both models from orienting the camera frame. A median shoulder width below 1 px, an unstable mean shoulder direction or a direction too close to vertical also stops forecasting. The orientation uses the average direction towards the contralateral shoulder and a perpendicular pointing towards the top of the image. The origin is the final midpoint between the shoulders.

A missing wrist or elbow can leave one ridge forecast usable. The Transformer needs both complete arms, even with attention blocked across arms. A missing future wrist removes that error measurement. A missing earlier donor removes the wrong-partner comparison. The remaining measurements keep their original positions.

Use a locally fitted checkpoint

The optional JSON checkpoint has the same format as cross_person_wrist/synthetic-fit.json in the companion NeuroTranslate package. Use that package’s cross_person_wrist.import_fit converter with compatible locally fitted source weights. Its four ridge heads, velocity gains, training scales, two sets of movement priors and fifteen trained networks must stay together. The reader validates coefficient shapes before use.

The browser runs the prediction in this tab. It does not upload the file or save it to browser storage. “Restore the fictional example” clears the local frames and local weights from the display. Your original files stay on your device.
