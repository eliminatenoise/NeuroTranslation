Download synthetic window (JSON)

  • grip_n and load_n each hold 200 samples at 500 Hz. Grip is the average normal force across the two plates, positive under compression. Load is the signed lift-axis force.
  • position holds 200 sets of coordinates for the object, index finger, thumb and wrist, in that order. Keep XYZ in the source scale.
  • eeg holds 200 rows of 32 channels. emg holds 1,600 rows of five channels at 4 kHz. Use the released voltage scales and channel order shown below.
  • time_from_cutoff_s holds the two clocks in seconds relative to cutoff. Keep samples oldest first. End the 500 Hz clock at zero and end EMG at its latest sample at or before zero.
  • issue_offset counts 500 Hz samples after the go cue and takes values 120, 140, …, 680. Use the observed trial_number within the series_type of weight, friction or mixed. The source schedule has 34 trials per weight or friction series and 28 per mixed series.

New devices, tasks and cohorts need their own validation. The browser checks the supplied shape and clocks. The file preparer supplies sensor order, scaling and real synchronization.

The original study recorded instrumented pinch lifts by healthy volunteers. Filter delay and clock drift limit what 120 ms on the recording clock says about live timing. The three final-test participants remain reserved.
