From stretch to spikes

As a rat muscle was stretched, a sensory nerve fiber fired. Instruments recorded the pull on the tendon, the position of the apparatus and the times of the nerve impulses, called spikes. How many spikes will arrive in the next 20 milliseconds?

ForecastCount error (MAE)Poisson deviance

Count error is the average absolute difference between predicted and measured counts. Poisson deviance also penalizes confident forecasts that miss. A smaller value is better for both measures.

What was measured?

A muscle spindle is a sensory organ within muscle. The recording follows one of its sensory nerve fibers during passive stretch. The apparatus position describes imposed muscle–tendon displacement. The force channel measures the pull on the tendon. File-specific conversion to physical force and distance units remains open. This study uses the values as released.
