Force history forecast better in first DEV

On 19 November 2026, the unchanged force-history model averaged 0.000360 V error across three DEV participants. Aligned EEG raised it to 0.000461 V and increased error for all three. The comparison is small, its descriptive interval includes zero, and FINAL remains reserved.

The seven TRAIN folds used to select penalties also favoured force history on average. The browser runs those frozen TRAIN fits. Its invented example demonstrates the input format.

The forecast uses recorded transducer voltage on the released timeline. The study archive supplies no verified calibration in newtons or physical synchronization for live use.

Source cohort by Pablo Ortega, Tong Zhao and A. Aldo Faisal. Study · Data deposit
