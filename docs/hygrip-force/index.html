Recent voltage gave the smaller error

Across three development participants, recent voltage and cue timing gave a mean error of 0.360 mV. Adding aligned scalp EEG raised it to 0.461 mV. Error increased for each participant. With three people, the descriptive interval for that change includes zero. The final cohort remains reserved.

The browser runs the models fitted on seven training participants. Force history also gave the lower average error when each training participant took a turn as the test case.

A secondary analysis added forearm EMG for those same three development participants. The mean error fell, but shifting the muscle signal in time gave a smaller error still. Read the timing controls and results.

The forecast uses recorded transducer voltage on the released timeline. The study archive supplies no verified calibration in newtons or physical synchronization for live use.
