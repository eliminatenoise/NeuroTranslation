Import JSON or CSV, inspect the samples and their declared sources, and align recordings that share the required metadata. The included example uses invented signals to demonstrate the software.

This data companion runs in your browser. It does not run a trained NeuroTranslate model, decode neural activity into movement, or provide a biological or clinical prediction. Your recording files are processed locally; the application has no recording-upload endpoint.
