# The website

This folder is served as [eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/START.html). Everything in it runs in the browser. A recording you open is read on your own computer and goes nowhere.

| Page | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [`START.html`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/START.html) | The start page. The finding so far, invented examples to try, and a list that matches each open dataset to the page that reads it. |
| [`index.html`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/index.html) | The app. It shows a recording on its own clock, lines several recordings up on a shared clock, and replays a palm trial against the fitted palm models. |
| Fourteen folders, from `cemhsey-arrays/` to `wrist-replay/` (excluding the other readers listed below) | The study readers of the numbered table, one per folder. Each reads a recording of the kind one study used and runs that study's forecasts on it, or shows the study's results. |
| [`hand-search/`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/hand-search/) | Type a sentence and find the moment of a recorded hand movement it describes, with the fitted models of [a newer study](../benchmarks/hand-language-retrieval/). Its [`forecast.html`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/hand-search/forecast.html) forecasts how far a wrist moves in the second after an instructor stops speaking ([its study](../benchmarks/preceding-instructions/)). |
| [`shared-motion/`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/shared-motion/) | The general model's shared fit, which forecasts how far a tracked wrist or rat moves in about a quarter of a second, and the two angles of a wrist from its movement, with forearm and scalp signals if they are given, with or without a correction from four forearm signals, both knee angles of a walker from four seconds of earlier angles, the turning moment at each knee from four seconds of leg angles, the force under a walker's right foot, where one marker on the skin will be 200 milliseconds later, and how three sensors on an arm change after an instruction such as "bend my right elbow", fitted on one person or on seven, with an optional correction from a person's earlier run or from early scalp and eye readings: eight tasks in all ([the model](../models/README.md)). |
| [`walking-landmarks/`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/walking-landmarks/) | Where will six skin markers go? Change the speed, the step rate or the muscle signals of an invented walker and see where six forecasts put both ankles, knees and wrists 200 milliseconds later, in 3D. [Try the shared model on one marker](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/shared-motion/#point-motion) · [See recorded marker travel](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/Interoceptomics/stride-signals/) · [Offline viewer](../models/walking-landmarks.zip) |
| [`elbow-cue/`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/elbow-cue/) | The elbow after a cue to bend or straighten it: compare a forecast of the elbow sensor from the cue and its recent readings with one that also reads scalp electrode C3. To give the shared model an instruction to move the right arm, [type a cue](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/shared-motion/#cued-arm). [Read the study](papers/cued-arm/) · [Find the sensors in Interoceptomics](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/Interoceptomics/scalp-and-arm/) · [Offline page](elbow-cue-offline.zip) |
| [`arm-transfer/`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/arm-transfer/) | Cued arm movements in new people: compare a small transformer's forecasts of three sensors, and the average change for each cue, with what five people outside the fitting group did. [Try a first-run correction](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/shared-motion/#arm-transfer) · [Read the study](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/papers/arm-transfer/) · [Offline reader](arm-transfer-offline.zip) |
| [`visual-balance/`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/visual-balance/) | Alter an invented movement or electrode signal to forecast the next 125 ms of a beam walk. [Offline reader](visual-balance-offline.zip) |
| [`visual-balance-controls/`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/visual-balance-controls/) | Compare movement alone with scalp, eye and muscle signals in NeuroTranslate 0.0. |
| [`papers/visual-balance/`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/papers/visual-balance/) | See how the forecasts fared on six independent walkers. |
| [`walking-instructions/`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/walking-instructions/) | Change a cue and forecast pelvis and heel displacement. [Compare NeuroTranslate 0.0](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/walking-instruction-controls/) · [Study](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/papers/walking-instructions/) · [Offline reader](walking-instructions-offline.zip) |
| [`heel-and-force/`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/heel-and-force/) | Forecast the motion of a right heel marker and the load under that foot. [Try NeuroTranslate 0.0](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/shared-heel-and-force/) · [Study](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/papers/heel-and-force/) · [Offline reader](heel-and-force-offline.zip) |
| [`pedal-load/`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/pedal-load/) | A pedal that cannot move: change an invented trace of its load and of the shin muscle's electrical signal, swap in another recording's muscle signal, and compare forecasts of the pedal's reading a tenth of a second later, with the errors on two new people. [Try the general model](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/shared-pedal-load/) · [Study](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/papers/pedal-load/) · [Offline reader](pedal-load-offline.zip) |
| [`shared-pedal-load/`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/shared-pedal-load/) | The same forecast from the general model: its shared part as it was, with a final step fitted for the pedal, beside straight-line fits, an untrained copy and a response model. Change the pedal's trend and the size and timing of a muscle burst. [Study](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/papers/pedal-load/) |
| [`muscle-tracking/`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/muscle-tracking/) | A muscle in the shin, filmed by ultrasound: choose or type an invented history of a bundle's length and angle, swap in other angles, and compare eight forecasts of how much longer or shorter it will look ten tracking steps later, with the errors on four new people. [Try the general model](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/shared-muscle-tracking/) · [Study](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/papers/muscle-tracking/) · [Offline reader](muscle-tracking-offline.zip) |
| [`shared-muscle-tracking/`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/shared-muscle-tracking/) | The same forecast from the general model: its shared part as it was, with a final step fitted for the shin muscle, beside straight-line fits, an untrained copy and a calculation from the bundle's geometry. Change an invented length and angle history. [Study](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/papers/muscle-tracking/) |
| [`back-rotation/`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/back-rotation/) | A sensor on the back: choose walking or standing and the readings the model may use, change an invented history, and see a small transformer's forecast of how many degrees the sensor will turn in the next tenth of a second, with and without two grids of electrodes on the skin over the spine, then the errors on three new people. [Try the general model](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/shared-back-rotation/) · [Study](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/papers/back-rotation/) · [Offline reader](back-rotation-offline.zip) |
| [`shared-back-rotation/`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/shared-back-rotation/) | The same forecast from the general model: its shared part as it was, with a final step fitted for the back sensor, beside carrying the last turn forward and a straight-line fit. Change an invented history of turns and voltages. [Study](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/papers/back-rotation/) |
| [`cervical-forecast/`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/cervical-forecast/) | The neck after a pulse at the wrist: change the early readings of an invented example and compare a small transformer's forecasts of the neck electrode's voltage 12 to 14 milliseconds after the pulse, with and without the recording at the armpit, then see how the forecasts did on six new people. [Study](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/papers/cervical-forecast/) · [Offline reader](cervical-forecast-offline.zip) |
| [`neck-voltage/`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/neck-voltage/) | The same forecast as the general model's program runs it: a straight-line correction to the last neck reading, from the neck alone, with the scalp, thumb and chest, or with the armpit as well. [Study](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/papers/cervical-forecast/) |
| [`scalp-forecast/`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/scalp-forecast/) | Scalp and eye readings in arm forecasts: compare forecasts of three arm sensors from the cue and early movement, with and without six scalp electrodes or three around the eyes, with the recorded test. [Try the eye adjustment](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/shared-motion/#early-signals) · [Read the study](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/papers/scalp-forecast/) · [Offline reader](scalp-forecast-offline.zip) |
| [`finger-transfer/`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/finger-transfer/) | Does a finger forecast carry over to a new person? Run the five-person model, which groups the brain-surface electrodes by the brain area each sat over, and see each person's scores from the turn in which they were the one left out. [Offline reader and model](../models/finger-transfer.zip) |
| [`walking-force/`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/walking-force/) | One stride, two force plates: change an invented muscle signal and compare six fitted models of the load two plates in the floor record as a person walks. [Right-foot plate model](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/shared-motion/#right-plate) · [Study and figures](papers/muscle-and-floor/) · [PDF](papers/muscle-and-floor/muscle-and-floor.pdf) · [Offline viewer](../models/walking-force.zip) |
| [`knee-moment/`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/knee-moment/) | What turning moment follows a step? Estimate how hard the forces around each knee push it to bend or straighten, from the previous four seconds of joint angles. [Offline reader](../models/knee-moment.zip) |
| [`finger-motion/`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/finger-motion/) | Change the finger named on the screen and an invented brain-surface rhythm, and see how six fitted models of one person's glove forecast how much each finger will move. [Offline reader](../models/finger-motion.zip) |
| [`finger-signals/`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/finger-signals/) | Give invented glove readings, a finger cue and a level of brain-surface activity to two fitted models of one person's glove, one of which also reads the brain signal, and see how each forecasts the five fingers 120 ms ahead; beside them, what adding the brain recording did on the recorded trials. [Offline reader](../models/finger-signals.zip) |
| [`knee-stride/`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/knee-stride/) | Follow both knees through four seconds of an invented walk, see which part of the previous stride the forecast copies, and compare the corrections trained for the knees with the general model's, 15 frames ahead. Then reveal the angles that followed, or open a recorded walk of the study, which the page checks before reading. [Offline reader](../models/knee-stride.zip) |
| [`gait-rhythm/`](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/gait-rhythm/) | Compare dominant leg-muscle and knee-angle rhythms for 11 walks, or calculate the spectra from compatible local recordings. [Offline reader and study](../models/gait-rhythm.zip) |

| `preview.html` | An old address. It forwards to the start page. |

The app is built from [`demo/`](../demo/README.md). Change it there and rebuild; do not edit the built files here. [`build-manifest.json`](build-manifest.json) names the build this folder holds.

Offline copies of the readers are listed in [LAUNCHERS.md](../LAUNCHERS.md); `hand-search/`, `shared-motion/`, `whole-body/` and `word-motion/` each open offline as a single saved page, with launchers in their folders. The stride and three-rat downloads also include offline viewers and launchers, and so do the ZIPs of `walking-landmarks/`, `elbow-cue/`, `arm-transfer/`, `scalp-forecast/`, `cervical-forecast/`, `back-rotation/`, `muscle-tracking/` and `pedal-load/`. The numbers behind them are on [the studies page](../benchmarks/README.md). Who made each recording, and under which licence: [RIGHTS.md](RIGHTS.md).
