    How did the model perform?

    The fit used earlier trips from one rat and was tested on later trips from the same rat and on a second animal. Its five fitted numbers were shared across the cells. The second animal had 122 local units. No unit had to be matched to the first animal.

    In the second rat, the model’s first choice was correct about 19.49% of the time. A rule that counted earlier spikes reached 19.48%. The fitted model assigned better probabilities overall, while often missing less active units. Only bins with one later spike gave a single correct identity. The recordings tell us about detected spikes and provide no way to identify an undetected spike.
