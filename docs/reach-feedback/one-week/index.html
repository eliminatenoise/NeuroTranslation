Does the update travel?

Each loss is the average Poisson deviance per contact for the same 100 ms of counts. Lower is better. First average windows within a recorded reach sequence, then give each supported sequence equal weight. The two workspaces are scored separately.

How the uncertainty was estimated

Subtract the comparison loss from the updated loss on the same supported windows. A negative difference favours the update. For each 95% interval, resample runs of eight consecutive original sequences 5,000 times within the same workspace and recording block. Unsupported sequences keep their place in the resampling order. Success requires the intervals for both comparisons to lie strictly below zero in both workspaces.

What can a contact tell us?

An electrode can keep its channel number while the events assigned to it change. The primary model adds every exported event on each of 37 contacts that carried an unsorted column on both days. Sort 0 means unsorted. A positive sort groups events with similar waveforms within a session. The authors’ records cannot identify a neuron across the week.

The 17-contact analysis retains events from local positive sorts on contacts with at least one positive-sort column in every block on both dates. A contact with no positive-sort column has no observation for that comparison. A zero count on a represented contact means no exported events in that interval. Contact 67 had no unsorted column in the training recording and contributes only where the positive-sort definition permits it.

The optical reconstruction, handle filtering and spike sorting were done offline. The order of samples lets us ask which predictions travel across a week in this animal. A cortical association alone cannot distinguish sensory input from a movement-related signal or establish the cause of either.

Recording & reuse

Raeed H. Chowdhury, Joshua I. Glaser and Lee E. Miller recorded these reaches. Their Dryad data carry a CC0 dedication. NeuroTranslation fitted the models and selected the excerpts. September 7 supplied 519 training windows from 198 rewarded originals in workspace 1. September 14 supplied the test.
