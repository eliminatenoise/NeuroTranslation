# Source and reuse

Céline Schreiber and Florent Moissenet collected the walking recordings and published the acquisition and processing methods. The version 8 dataset is available under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. [Recordings](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.7734767.v8) · [Scientific Data article](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-019-0124-4) · [CC BY 4.0](CC-BY-4.0.txt)

NeuroTranslation used two protocol instructions, “as usual” and “fast but not running”. We derived a pelvis centre and right-heel trajectory, rotated each history into the direction of its recent travel, fitted Transformers to predict 100 ms displacements and calculated aggregate errors. The interactive examples are invented. The original recordings remain with their authors.

NeuroTranslation dedicates its own contributions to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal to the extent of its rights. [CC0 legal text](LICENSE)
