Forecasting variation in a zebrafish’s tail angle

Carrying forward the previous tenth of a second of tail-angle variation gave the smallest average error across 2,268 forecasts in one recording. Adding the logged direction of the visual pattern to the fitted camera rule raised its average error by 0.000572 radians.
