What goes into a forecast?
Stored clock
Each case has a target time after the cue and a last-known angle on each axis. The last-known angles are 128 stored ticks (nominally 250 ms at 512 Hz) before the target. The 256 stored samples through that last-known instant form 16 bins of 31.25 ms each. The wrist encoders refreshed at a reported nominal 100 Hz. Physical delays remain unmeasured here.
Feature values before normalization
Each bin has two mean wrist angles relative to the last-known angles, in degrees. The four forearm values are root-mean-square (RMS) amplitudes of software differences between two surface electromyography (EMG) channels per pair, in nominal archive µV. The eight scalp electroencephalography (EEG) means follow C3, C1, Cz, C2, C4, CP3, CPz and CP4 in archive scaling. Surface EMG can include neighboring muscle activity and effects of electrode contact or motion.
Values the fitted models use
Each model subtracts the training mean and divides by the training standard deviation for each bin feature and each last-known angle. These standardized inputs are unitless. One of six timing indicators is set to 1 for the target cue offset. The models predict a standardized change on each axis, then undo the target scaling and add the last-known angles to get absolute degrees.

The browser uses the released ridge weights and training scales and the invented feature cases. It accepts only those cases and the M1 adjustment.

What happened on recorded trials?

6.530° → 6.174°

Adding surface EMG lowered the two-axis forecast error by 0.355° using the full-precision scores. Giving that same model EMG from the next eligible trial raised the error to 6.941°. That swap kept the participant, recording block and cue offset fixed. Adding scalp EEG after EMG lowered the overall error by a further 0.048° and raised the radial–ulnar error by 0.011°.

Six participants supplied the fits. Three different participants supplied 960 development trials. The six reserved test participants remain sealed. Changing an invented example above leaves these archived scores fixed.
