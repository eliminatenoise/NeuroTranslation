# Source and reuse

Qiang Zhang, Noor Hakam, Oluwasegun Akinniyi, Ashwin Iyer, Xuefeng Bao and Nitin Sharma released *AnkleImage – An ultrafast ultrasound image dataset to understand the ankle joint muscle contractility* under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. The study records people trying to lift the forefoot against a fixed pedal. [AnkleImage data](https://doi.org/10.6019/S-BIAD1482) · [Original article](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-024-04285-x) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) · [Licence text](CC-BY-4.0.txt)

NeuroTranslation used muscle and load-cell channels from Task 1 to fit models and calculate forecast errors. This package includes fitted parameters, aggregate errors and invented example inputs. The researchers distribute the original recordings through AnkleImage.
