# Pedal load · NeuroTranslation

Extract the full archive and keep its files together. Open the launcher named for Windows, macOS or Linux. You can also double-click **index.html** in a browser that supports JavaScript. The model and its results work offline without installing a runtime or starting a server. Links to the online companions and source archive require a connection.

Choose an invented load history, pedal setting and forecast method. Enter a multiplier to change the original muscle signal’s amplitude, or edit the 201 individual readings in each trace. The alternate trace lets you try the retrospective comparison while keeping the original pedal load and setting fixed. Apply edited readings to calculate the forecast. The plot ends at one predicted reading 100 samples beyond the cutoff. The example supplies no measured value for that future point.

Two development participants supplied the fitted weights. Two other Task 1 volunteers were reserved for assessment. An unresolved right-load column and time grid in one neutral trial prevented the planned two-person test. The reader reports the complete volunteer, the other volunteer’s two angle5 trials and that volunteer’s one usable neutral trial separately. The neutral trial has no usable other-trial muscle signal. The two muscle inputs in the retrospective comparison came from separate trials by the same person at the same setting. The channel recorded by the load sensor has no verified force calibration for this analysis. All forecasts and errors use native recording units. At the nominal 1,000-sample-per-second acquisition rate, 100 samples span 100 milliseconds. Physical sample intervals and dropped readings remain unverified.
