# Sources and reuse

Desirée Irene Gracia Laso, Eduardo Iáñez, Mario Ortiz and José M. Azorín released *An Electrospinography Database of Spinal Cord Activity During Gait-Related Tasks and Motor Imagery Exercises*, version 2, under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. The study describes the equipment, electrode placement and tasks. [Dataset](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19065545) · [Original study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-026-07592-7) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) · [Local licence text](CC-BY-4.0.txt)

NeuroTranslation used the recorded orientations and unfiltered electrode histories to train separate models and calculate forecast errors. The package contains fitted parameters, aggregate scores and invented inputs. The source recordings remain available from the authors.
