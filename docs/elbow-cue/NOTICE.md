# Source and licences

Patrick Ofner, Andreas Schwarz, Joana Pereira and Gernot R. Müller-Putz (Graz University of Technology) released *Upper limb movement decoding from EEG*, version 1 (2017). The publisher credits these four authors and applies Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Cite the recordings and their acquisition paper when reusing the derived materials. The authors supplied the recordings and the event and channel definitions. [Original dataset](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.834976) · [Acquisition paper](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0182578) · [Original codebook](https://zenodo.org/records/834976/files/data%20set%20description.pdf)

The NeuroTranslation project selected two event classes from three execution runs of one participant, summarized elbow, scalp and eye channels, fitted candidate models and calculated aggregate forecast errors. The reader plots user-editable invented values. Data-derived material retains attribution under CC BY 4.0. [CC BY 4.0 text](DATA-LICENCE.txt)

To the extent of its rights, the project dedicates its original reader, analysis software, prose and invented example under CC0 1.0 Universal. The plot labels and geometric symbols are drawn locally. The underlying recordings remain available from their authors. [CC0 text](LICENSE)
