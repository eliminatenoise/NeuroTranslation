# Choose the model by what it has to predict

A moving arm leaves several records. Electrodes can record electrical activity in nerve or muscle. Cameras can locate the wrist. A load cell can record the force applied to a handle. A physics model can estimate how a tendon path changes with posture. A forecast becomes testable when we specify which earlier records it can see, which later measurement it must predict, and which animal or person supplies the independent test.

**Representation** describes what the numbers stand for, including the tissue, sensor, reference frame and units. **Context** tells us which earlier measurements, body geometry, task and contact conditions were available at the time. **Grounding** comes from comparing a forecast with an independent observation of the quantity that matters. A simulator can enforce its equations and still give the wrong answer for a real arm.

## Combining signals

The proposed system first joins neural activity with muscle observations, and measured displacement with a physics model. It then joins the outputs of those two branches. The design keeps a nerve electrode distinct from surface EMG, the electrical activity measured in muscle fibers. Mechanical contractile state covers quantities such as muscle tension, force and fascicle shortening. EMG can inform an estimate of those quantities. The measurement itself remains electrical. Store the EMG stream once, with its clock, units, location and provenance; both branches can reference it. To test what it adds, remove or misalign the underlying observation in every place where it contributes.

The return direction needs the same care. An earlier movement can help forecast a later cortical signal. To test peripheral sensory input directly, use a recording of activity traveling from the body towards the nervous system, such as an identified muscle-spindle afferent. Relating displacement to a later cortical count alone leaves the intervening route uncertain. Where no data share the same trial clock, a separate model or a shared representation can still be useful. Joining those recordings into a fabricated measured event would invalidate the test.

## Three ways to generate a future

| Architecture | What happens at inference | Relevant task and test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Autoregressive Transformer | Predict the next event or segment using the observed past and earlier generated outputs. | A natural candidate for ordered spike events and fast streaming. Measure cumulative errors when it must use its own earlier guesses and compare with an inexpensive firing-history rule. |
| Diffusion Transformer | Repeatedly remove noise from a possible future, conditioned on the same recorded past. | Useful when several whole movement trajectories remain plausible. Score both positional accuracy and the distribution of outcomes, and count every denoising pass towards latency. |
| Autoregressive–diffusion hybrid | Generate successive blocks in order, using a denoiser within each block. | A candidate for action-conditioned motion and simultaneous channels. Compare it with both simpler families on the same data, forecast horizons and compute settings. Longer plausible sequences alone do not establish physiological accuracy. |

World Labs calls **Atlas** a multimodal autoregressive diffusion transformer. It conditions on spatial information including images, camera poses and depth. That is a strong design reference for aligning views and reconstructing a moving scene. A rendered view does not establish the neural or muscular source of that movement. The original Genie used an autoregressive dynamics model, while DeepMind described Genie 2 as an autoregressive latent diffusion model. These are related but distinct examples. The small open comparison in this release trains mechanisms in this family on actual recordings; it makes no claim to reproduce the proprietary systems or their performance. ## What to take from the history

IBM’s Watson generated candidate answers and weighed evidence for new Jeopardy clues. AlphaGo’s Move 37 against Lee Sedol illustrated how search and learned policies can produce a move experts did not expect. AlphaZero later combined self-play with the rules of chess, shogi and Go. For our purposes, a game’s rules and outcome provide an unusually clear way to check a proposed action. The consequence of a muscle force estimate is harder to check. It needs an appropriate independent instrument. [IBM’s DeepQA account](https://research.ibm.com/publications/building-watson-an-overview-of-the-deepqa-project) · [AlphaGo and Move 37](https://deepmind.google/research/alphago/) · [AlphaZero](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphazero-shedding-new-light-on-chess-shogi-and-go/)

The original Transformer showed that attention could learn useful relationships across a sequence and improve machine translation. Reasoning models later used reinforcement learning to improve performance on tasks where outcomes can be checked. A verifiable training reward applies to what its checker can measure. A correct numerical answer, a passing program, a plausible animation and a correctly predicted physical force have different checkers. Factual errors can persist in reasoning models. Here the most valuable verifier is a later neural, muscular, mechanical or optical measurement held out at the correct biological level.
