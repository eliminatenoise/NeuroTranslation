What the sensors recorded

The study followed a supported right arm during six instructed movements and rest. Sensors measured elbow motion, forearm rotation and bending near the base of the index finger. The experiment uses those three readings and the known instruction. A rise on a sensor’s stored scale has no verified conversion here to degrees of joint motion.

There are three forecast times, measured from a cutoff 0.25 seconds after the cue. Each target subtracts the final early median from a later eight-sample median. Only those endpoints were scored. A direction, path through space or individual prediction interval cannot be read from the lines.
