Patrick Ofner, Andreas Schwarz, Joana Pereira and Gernot R. Müller-Putz at Graz University of Technology published *Upper limb movements can be decoded from the time-domain of low-frequency EEG* in 2017. Their version 1 data release contains recordings, movement cue markers and the source codebook. The authors request that users cite the accompanying paper. [Data and source codebook](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.834976) · [Acquisition paper](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0182578)

NeuroTranslation selected motor-execution runs, summarized three mechanical channels relative to the cue, fitted prediction models and calculated forecast errors. The supplied model weights, training summaries and aggregate errors derive from the source recordings and retain attribution under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. The interactive starting example combines invented readings derived from training summaries. This edition contains no participant recording or trial trajectory. [CC BY 4.0 legal text](CC-BY-4.0.txt)
