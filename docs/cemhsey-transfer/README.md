# Practice a different pinch

Extract the full ZIP to a folder. Open the launcher for Windows, macOS or Linux, or open `index.html` in a current Chromium-based browser. The example runs offline. File and fit checks use the browser’s SHA-256 support. Compressed MATLAB files also need its deflate decoder.

Choose 10%, 30% or 50% of maximum force. Move the cutoff in repeat 2. The tab shows the last force reading, two cue-based controls and both frozen and adjusted model forecasts. Fit on repeat 1 to add two adapted forecasts. Reveal the gauge reading at the selected target, or score the whole comparison repeat to see its 592 errors summarized together. The supplied signals and weights are invented.

## Try two recordings on your own device

Use adult CEMHSEY GRASP pad-to-pad pinch, Session 5. Task 1 uses 10%, Task 2 uses 30% and Task 3 uses 50%. Choose two distinct repeats from the same participant, day and task. Use each file in its corresponding role. Check the originals before you confirm a relabelled pair. The reader checks filename consistency and duplicate decoded readings. A filename or a different fingerprint cannot establish that two files come from the asserted sources.

Use compatible MATLAB Level 5 originals with normalized `data_force`, a 320 × 61,500 double `data_sEMG` matrix and positive scalar `MVC`. If you have a compatible 30% model JSON from the [array reader](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/cemhsey-arrays/fit/), you can open it here. Check its training sources against your selected repeats. Where the fit includes training fingerprints, the reader rejects exact training reuse in either fitted practice or comparison. The fit’s origin label comes from its JSON metadata. Your selections and practice coefficients stay in this tab until you reload or close it.

The gauge measures force between two digits as one normalized reading. Scores are descriptive errors for the selected second repeat. The 120 ms forecast lead uses the nominal sample indices and gives no estimate of device latency.
