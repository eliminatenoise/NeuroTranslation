What practice can change

The frozen models keep their 30% weights. The adjusted forecasts add the difference in target strength times the cue’s change over the next 120 nominal milliseconds. That adjustment is zero at 30%. The practice repeat gives each model one more coefficient on the same cue change. The comparison repeat supplies no fitted values.

Each score describes this repeat alone. The study reports separate results across its evaluation panel. The gauge gives one normalized pinch force. The grids sample muscle electrical activity through the skin. Neither yields separate force for each digit. The 120 ms lead uses nominal release indices.
