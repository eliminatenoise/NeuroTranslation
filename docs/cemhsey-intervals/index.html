How wide is a pinch forecast?

One band keeps the same width through a pinch. The other changes width as the cue moves from rest to ramp, hold and release. Both were calibrated to cover 90% of later gauge readings. Here’s how often they did on two other people.

What counts as one forecast?

The force recorder supplies one later reading per forecast. Each of eight evaluation trials contributes 592 cutoffs, 50 milliseconds apart. The target lies 24 force samples ahead, or 120 milliseconds on the released recording index. Adjacent forecasts overlap. The 4,736 cutoffs come from two people and do not act as 4,736 independent replications.

Eight other recordings from four people set the widths. Each interval refers to one later reading. A whole trial can leave its band even when most of its individual readings fall inside.
