# Heel & force

Extract the full archive. Open the launcher for Windows, macOS or Linux, or double-click **index.html**. A current browser with JavaScript runs the fitted weights on your computer. No installation, connection or local server is needed. Web references and companion links require a connection.

Use the sliders to change the invented right heel and muscle histories. The arrows show the joint model’s and heel specialist’s predicted changes in XYZ position over the coming 0.10 s. Read the force chart for the joint model’s and force specialist’s estimates of the upward plate load at that same future instant. The solid and dashed lines point to predicted endpoints. The movement between endpoints was not reconstructed. Choose a different projection to see a component hidden by the viewing angle.

Select **Markers & time only** to run a separately fitted joint model that received no muscle signal during training. Both specialists still use the muscle signal. Pattern B is another invented example. The study’s recorded-muscle swap used different repetitions by the same person at the same elapsed time. See **Study results** for the donor coverage and separate errors on recorded measurements.

Open **Edit marker positions and muscle readings** to change individual measurements. Arrays list the earliest observations first. Enter 24 frames, with heel then forefoot XYZ for each frame, four bins of eight muscle amplitudes, and an available elapsed offset in 100 Hz frames. Examples assume that the right foot stays on a measured plate through the target block. Editing them cannot establish that the foot will remain in contact. The model can be numerically evaluated outside the familiar signal range; the study supplies no accuracy guarantee there.
