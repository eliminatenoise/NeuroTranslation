# Source and reuse

Florent Moissenet and Céline Schreiber released the v8 multimodal gait recordings under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. Their data descriptor and supplement document the motion-capture markers, muscle electrodes, gait events, force plates and foot assignments. The linked acquisition and processing belong to the original authors. [Dataset v8](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.7734767.v8) · [Schreiber & Moissenet, Scientific Data](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-019-0124-4) · [Author processing code](https://github.com/fmoissenet/CGA_Rehazenter_Toolbox/tree/e35dc90c1acd24da134897fd1b4241152cff6005) · [CC BY 4.0 legal text](CC-BY-4.0.txt)

NeuroTranslation derived paired 100 ms heel displacement and upward-force targets from the same foot and recorded walk, extracted marker histories and muscle-voltage summaries, fitted the four predictors, and calculated their aggregate errors. The reader distributes fitted parameters, inference code, aggregate results and invented examples. The source recordings remain available from the original authors.

The NeuroTranslation project dedicates its own contributions to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal to the extent of its rights. [CC0 legal text](LICENSE)
