What the model sees

Take the median channel amplitude in each grid, transform it with log(1 + RMS in mV), and subtract the mean of the five transformed medians. A positive contrast places the grid above that window’s mean.

Source and timing

Cell positions follow the numbering on each acquisition grid. The maps show electrode positions within each grid. Identifying an individual muscle or nerve requires separate evidence.

The force channel is one dynamometer reading in fractions of that day’s maximum voluntary contraction (MVC) for this grasp. Twenty-four force ticks span a nominal 120 ms. The timing between the force and EMG acquisition clocks was unverified. Band widths describe errors in offline forecasts of the gauge reading.
