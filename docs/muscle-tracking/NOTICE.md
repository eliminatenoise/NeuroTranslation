# AnkleImage and reuse

Qiang Zhang, Noor Hakam, Oluwasegun Akinniyi, Ashwin Iyer, Xuefeng Bao and Nitin Sharma released *AnkleImage – An ultrafast ultrasound image dataset to understand the ankle joint muscle contractility* under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. Their study documents how they recorded volunteers moving the ankle. [Source data](https://doi.org/10.6019/S-BIAD1482) · [Original study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-024-04285-x) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) · [Licence text](CC-BY-4.0.txt)

NeuroTranslation used the tracked lengths and angles from Task 3 to fit prediction models and calculate errors. This reader includes the fitted parameters, aggregate results and invented input histories. The authors’ source recordings are available from the original archive.
