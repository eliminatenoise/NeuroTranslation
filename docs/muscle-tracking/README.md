# Muscle tracking · NeuroTranslation

Extract the full archive, then open the launcher for Windows, macOS or Linux. You can also open **index.html** directly in a browser with JavaScript. Keep the extracted files together. Running a forecast requires no connection, installation or local server.

Choose a history or enter 21 invented lengths in millimetres and 20 earlier angles in degrees. An alternate set of 20 angles lets you change the angle input while keeping the lengths fixed. Choose a forecast method, or open the comparison to see all eight forecasts from the same history. Negative change means shortening. The graph shows one predicted length ten tracker steps after the cutoff. The intermediate future remains unknown.

The separate recording results compare the forecasts with later image-tracked lengths from the shin during seated ankle dorsiflexion. Imaging and muscle-voltage clocks could not be aligned frame by frame. Force and neural drive were outside this study. Offline tracking may use information acquired after a particular frame.
