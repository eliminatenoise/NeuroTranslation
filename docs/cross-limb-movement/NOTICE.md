# Source and reuse

Matthew D. Luciw, Ewa Jarocka and Benoni B. Edin released WAY-EEG-GAL under CC0. They measured the right wrist, arm and hand of volunteers reaching for and lifting an object after a light cue. NeuroTranslation uses the five named muscle channels and wrist marker under the study’s channel-quality and timing rules. The stored upper-limb muscle amplitude has no verified conversion into volts or microvolts. [Original study and data references](https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2014.47) · [Study supplement](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC4365902.1/sdata201447-s1.pdf)

Steven M. Peterson and Daniel P. Ferris released the Perturbed beam-walking task on OpenNeuro under CC0. NeuroTranslation uses the lower-leg electrical signals and sacrum marker during visual rotation in standing and walking sessions from version 1.0.3. [Versioned dataset](https://doi.org/10.18112/openneuro.ds003739.v1.0.3) · [Licence declaration](https://github.com/OpenNeuroDatasets/ds003739/blob/f15449b6aecb83044b49a1798955e8a212a68fc1/dataset_description.json) · [Collection methods](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2021.107635)

This reader distributes fitted model parameters, software, two invented histories and group-level errors. NeuroTranslation dedicates its contributions to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal to the extent of its rights. [CC0 legal text](LICENSE)
