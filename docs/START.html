The hand’s own history still beats the brain signal

NeuroTranslation tries to predict one recording of a movement from another: brain, muscle, force and motion, all from open datasets. In 25 small studies so far, what the hand had just done was the forecast to beat. Adding a brain recording taken at the scalp (EEG) made that forecast worse in 8 of 13 comparisons. Every study, with its numbers.

The pages on this site run the studies’ forecasts in your browser. Try an invented example, or open a recording from the dataset a study used.

Keep a launcher on your computer

These shortcuts open the app in your browser, at the part that replays a recorded palm movement. They need a network connection. If a shortcut opens as text, extract the ZIP first. Keep the filename extension and follow your system’s normal opening prompts.

  • Windows launcher
  • macOS launcher
  • Linux launcher
  • Linux menu installer
  • All launchers ZIP

For a Linux application-menu entry, extract the ZIP and run sh Install-NeuroTranslation-Linux.sh in that folder. An entry made by an earlier installer opens this page. Choose Open the palm replay under Find your recording. The installer leaves existing entries and your changes to them alone, and opens no browser.

Checked: the format of the Windows and macOS shortcuts, and the Linux installer in an isolated folder. Not checked: opening the shortcuts on a real Windows or macOS machine.

Run two measured recordings with Python

Both run on your own computer, not on this site. The general model’s first fit opens a page in your browser that forecasts five sensors on a walking person, on a walk it was fitted on, so it shows the model running, not how well it would forecast someone new. One wrist trial shows how a recording is labelled, channel by channel, for the general model. No model is fitted to it.

Two papers, each written twice

Two working drafts, each in a Technical and a Simple English edition. Nobody outside the project has reviewed them. Every result in them comes from stored recordings, and nothing is live. They do not yet contain the general model’s first fits, made on 20 November.
