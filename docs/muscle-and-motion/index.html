Human recordings

How close did the forecasts come?

Standing and walking were recorded in separate sessions. Standing came first for this type of visual perturbation. Offline filters and optical resampling used information beyond a single sample. The errors describe forecasts on qualifying recorded windows and leave live response timing open.
