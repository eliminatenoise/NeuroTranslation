# Sources and reuse

Steven M. Peterson and Daniel P. Ferris collected and released the *Perturbed beam-walking task*. The study uses version 1.0.3 of OpenNeuro ds003739, including the visual-rotation standing and walking sessions. The authors specify CC0 1.0. [Versioned dataset](https://doi.org/10.18112/openneuro.ds003739.v1.0.3) · [Dataset licence statement](https://github.com/OpenNeuroDatasets/ds003739/blob/f15449b6aecb83044b49a1798955e8a212a68fc1/dataset_description.json) · [Data collection and processing](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2021.107635) · [Original research article](https://doi.org/10.1523/ENEURO.0207-18.2018)

NeuroTranslation trained the joint and specialist Transformers on the authors’ recordings. This reader contains their fitted coefficients, inference code, one invented history and group-level evaluation results. An independent regularized linear model and the practical references supply the comparisons. All the interactive example traces are synthetic. The recorded group averages pool eligible people, postures and rotation directions.
