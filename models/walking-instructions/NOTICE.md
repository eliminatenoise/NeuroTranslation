# Source and reuse

Céline Schreiber and Florent Moissenet collected and published the walking recordings. The version 8 release is available under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. [Dataset](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.7734767.v8) · [Data descriptor](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-019-0124-4) · [CC BY 4.0 legal text](CC-BY-4.0.txt)

This study selected two task instructions, combined four surface markers to estimate the pelvis position, rotated marker histories into walking coordinates and calculated displacements over 100 ms. The learned weights and aggregate errors derive from those measurements. The example contains invented positions.
