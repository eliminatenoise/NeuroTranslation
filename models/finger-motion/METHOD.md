# Design fixed before inspecting the movement target

**Question.** In one familiar recording, do recent cortical measurements help estimate how far each of five glove readings will move during the next part of a finger-cue trial? Estimate mean absolute change per nominal 40 ms sensor step. At cutoff t take glove readings at t+40, +80, +120 and +160 ms, cast to float64 before subtraction, and calculate the arithmetic mean of the three successive absolute differences separately for thumb, index, middle, ring and little. Repeated readings count as zero change. This measures total variation over 120 ms divided by three. The public release places readings on a nominal 25 Hz grid. Independent packet timestamps and joint angle calibration are unavailable. No lag search, source clock correction or label-dependent sample selection.

**Recorded input.** Admit only the prepared 362,000-row FingerFlex prefix, SHA-256 57ea209841127b6cc04f2c03f8f6146f01b3e0235d169365d009cf3f9c643e9c. The chronological inner fit consists of complete cue-and-following-blank trials within [0,236000) source rows, and evaluation uses complete pairs within [244000,362000). Discard crossing trials, endpoint-truncated trials and the intervening guard. For both phases start at the next inclusive multiple of 40 ms after onset and add tick offsets 9 through 42 inclusive. Each trial must supply 34 cutoffs per phase with all predictors and future targets inside that phase. With equal 68 cutoffs per trial, equal row weights give equal complete trial weights. Physical clocks and display latency remain unresolved. The later source validation and test and all other people stay closed. The prior independent pilot used this same inner assessment for a different target; this is development evidence.

**Four-token packet.** At each of t−120, −80, −40 and t milliseconds, use the 160 preceding amplifier samples ending inclusively at that token, 46 contacts. Convert by 0.0298 µV/count, remove instantaneous common average, demean each window/channel, apply Hann taper and sum one-sided 1-kHz spectral density times bin width in 12–32 and 70–150 Hz inclusive, excluding 113.75–126.25 Hz in the upper band. Take log(1+power/1µV²), ordered band first, yielding 92 numbers. Encode ten cells for digit × display/blank phase, writing intercept, elapsed seconds and positive-part hinges at 0.4, 0.8, 1.2 and 1.6 s in the active cell, zero elsewhere. Blank retains the last displayed digit. Concatenate cue followed by cortex, shape (4,152); glove measurements are excluded from every main predictor, including normalization. The earlier published preprocessing code hashes are b150b14f6816c99c2d36ba2d57cb2a8e74cd70b1957ef547fe789877e735ba59 (partition) and 1f8d937eaab97c6bfcf68ba6b0f728dfefb2c818067204e5ccccb7bc23fa07ed (spectral and timing functions). The separate history-only ridge uses four past readings of all digits and their three successive absolute differences, flattened, width 35. Only this comparator receives a glove input.

**Controls and models.** Within each split and cue, use the previous eligible completed trial as the wrong cortical donor at the same phase/tick. No wraparound or cross-split donor. The first eligible trial for every cue is donor-only for every method. Fit the training-mean baseline and four ridge regressions (cue only, cortex only, aligned cue+cortex and separate glove history). Flatten the selected columns in token-major order, standardize each feature from fit rows, replacing SD below 1e−8 with one, unpenalized intercept, ridge penalty 0.05 times fit row count. Standardize each output using fit mean and SD. If a target has SD below 1e−8, stop the experiment. All predictions are returned in original units and floored at zero before scoring. At evaluation substitute the earlier cortical donor into the aligned fit with the real current cue and the same weights.

Train three matched one-block Transformer encoders (cue only, cortex only, aligned cue+cortex). Retain full 152 input width. Construct absent modalities with zeros after standardization using only the selected training features; excluded measurements never enter predictions or exported preprocessing parameters. Train-only input mean/SD are pooled over observed training tokens. Seed 319 for each initial network and identical deterministic shuffles. Project to 24, add four learned position vectors initialized at zero, apply 2-head self-attention, 48-wide GELU feedforward, post-norm, zero dropout. Read five standardized targets from the last token. All four tokens are available at t. Train 24 epochs, batches 128 including last partial batch, AdamW lr 0.001, weight decay 0.01, mean squared error, CPU float32, no tuning or checkpoint selection. Reuse the aligned weights for its same-earlier-cue evaluation. Report all outcomes for both families.

**Primary comparisons.** Use mean absolute error per finger in stored sensor units per 40 ms interval. Divide each error by its training target SD, then average equally over the five digits, 68 cutoffs and each whole trial. For each family report cue-only minus aligned and earlier-substituted minus aligned, plus cortex-only minus aligned and aligned ridge minus aligned Transformer. Report separate display/blank and chronological recipient counts by cue; packet counts are not independent observations. Paired circular moving-block bootstrap of consecutive trial triplets, drawn with replacement and trimmed to recipient count, 5000 draws seed 48019. Report percentile 95% intervals for aggregate and per-finger gains. Positive gain means lower aligned error.

**Conditional movement beyond the label.** Within the later internal evaluation recipients, form nonoverlapping chronological pairs of repeats of the same cue (first with second, third with fourth, etc.). Compare observed difference between trial two and trial one at each matched phase/tick with the difference between their predictions. Identical cue/phase/tick labels alone supply a zero difference; include the actual cue encoder too, since the grid can differ in elapsed time by up to 39 ms. For each family compare the aligned difference error against (i) zero difference, (ii) cue-only difference, (iii) earlier-substituted difference. Report support by cue and discarded odd repeats, and per-finger and macro gains. Sort pairs by later trial and bootstrap as above over nonoverlapping pairs (triplets, seed 48019). Infer a provisional within-cue advantage for the aligned Transformer only when both primary comparisons and all three conditional comparisons have positive lower 95% bounds, with >=6 pairs across >=3 cues. Report ridge against the same prespecified criteria separately. These descriptive within-record intervals cannot establish transfer, independent clock alignment or performance across patients. Explicitly report failure of any gate, no alternative endpoint.
