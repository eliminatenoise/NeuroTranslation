Does movement help across trials?

How to read the comparison

The model learns only from pushes on 12 September. It uses the first 322 eligible original trials from a block of 324, yielding 1,288 training windows. The later first-day block checks development. The following day tests transfer within the same animal. Both kinds of go cue fall outside the training task phase.

Each loss difference subtracts the comparison model’s Poisson deviance from the full Transformer’s. A negative value favours the full model. The analysis gives every contact equal weight, averages windows within each original trial, then averages across trials. The group result averages differences across three training seeds. The 95% intervals resample chronological blocks of 12 original trials 5,000 times. They describe variation across trials from these recordings.

The primary test requires both upper interval limits to fall below zero on matched next-day push windows. Comparing with counts and cues asks what movement adds. Substituting the nearest earlier original’s movement asks whether the right movement matters. Matching requires the same phase, event direction, offset and visible cue flags and directions. A future or missing donor cannot stand in.

Animal C, 12–13 September 2017. Area 2 of primary somatosensory cortex. These data test a forecast of recorded electrical events. Sensation, motor commands and accuracy in other animals remain unmeasured here.
