# Counting the next 100 milliseconds

The primary question is whether keeping the correct handle movement improves next-day contact-count forecasts after accounting for preceding cortical counts and visible task cues. The answer uses the pre-target bump phase on the held day. Both paired contrasts must favour the full Transformer. Each full-minus-comparator upper 95% endpoint must be strictly below zero for Poisson deviance. The two comparators are counts plus cues and the full model supplied with a different earlier original's movement. The latter retains the recipient's preceding counts and cues. Positive effects mean larger errors. Report the outcome of each comparison, including uncertainty or worsening. Other phases, strata, metrics, seeds, controls and the day-one validation sample are descriptive. Intervals are pointwise.

## Populations and losses

Use the frozen day-one training archive to compute per-contact training means, with equal weight per original and then per window within an original. Persistence copies the preceding 100-ms recorded count of that contact. Use exported predictions for both fitted model families. On every eligible window, report all methods with universal support. On the subset with an earlier donor, report all methods, including wrong movement. Never drop rows according to prediction values. Loss of a whole original or part of its windows is reported. Reconstruct the closest strictly earlier different trial with identical phase, actual direction, offset and the full five-token cue-flag/direction history. Donors and recipients belong to the same split. An unobserved donor yields undefined wrong-movement predictions on all seeds and contacts.

For observed count y and expected count p, report |y−p| in counts per contact per 100 ms. Let m=max(p,0.000001). The Poisson deviance is 2[y log(y/m)−y+m], with the logarithmic term zero for y=0. Also report observed and predicted mean counts and the number of positive counts paired with an exactly zero forecast. A positive count paired with zero has infinite unfloored deviance. Include measured zero-cell denominators. The floor affects only deviance.

For each phase, population and stratum, take an equal average over its selected named contacts and available windows within each original, then an equal average across contributing originals. Recompute this hierarchy for each subset. Compute errors separately for each Transformer seed (17, 41, 73) before averaging them with equal weight. Effects pair within the same original, window, contact and seed. Deterministic controls are repeated across seeds only for seed-specific differences. No days or phases are pooled. Direction is the recorded phase event direction, including when its cue was still hidden from the predictor. Offsets 0, 5, 10 and 15 source bins mean 0, 50, 100 and 150 ms after that phase's event.

## Chronological uncertainty

In each phase, retain the sorted unique original IDs in its pre-signal-QC metadata membership, including originals left with zero scored windows. Keep the full phase sequence when estimating direction, offset and electrode results. Each draw samples starts uniformly from index 0 through n−12 inclusive, with replacement. Concatenate non-circular blocks of 12 consecutive phase-eligible originals to n original slots and truncate the last block. Use NumPy default_rng (PCG64), seed 20170913, 5,000 replicates. Draw starts as a single row-major matrix with shape (5000,ceil(n/12)); reset the RNG for each chronology. Reuse the same draws for all arms on that chronology. All rows of an original move together. Excluded originals retain their slots but have zero contribution to numerator and denominator; skip and count any replicate without support. Compute paired differences within each draw. Take the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles with linear interpolation. A sensitivity calculation uses the same procedure on the complete assigned chronology (108 validation IDs or all 1,387 held-day IDs including phase-ineligible slots). It does not determine the primary decision. There is no cross-phase resampling of losses, circular wrap, seed resampling or pooling of two phases in one estimate. The fixed models, donor mapping, contacts and observed recording dates define the scope of these intervals.

## Files

- `summary.json` gives source hashes, per-phase support, aggregate score and contrast records under `phases.<phase>.<all_eligible|matched>`, per-seed results, the alternative timeline, both primary gate checks and the decision. A metric has `estimate`, `interval` and `draws`. Model IDs start with `transformer_` or `poisson_`. Controls are `training_mean` and `persistence`. Contrasts are named `<model>-minus-<comparator>`.
- `strata.csv.gz` gives seed-averaged scores and paired effects with intervals by phase, population, direction, offset, their intersection and contact. All original IDs contributing to a stratum keep their phase chronology.
- `original-scores.csv.gz` records per-original scores by phase, population and model, including every member of the pre-QC timeline. Blank metrics denote zero supported windows.
- `donors.csv.gz` lists pre-QC recipient keys, presence after QC and exact donor trial and row identity. Zero-based row indices refer to the emitted source archive.
- `RESULTS.md` gives the question, sample, outcomes and scope. Test and validation remain in separate directories.

Run with Python 3.11+ and NumPy using `python -B score.py --stage validation --train TRAIN.npz --source SOURCE_DIR --models MODELS_DIR --output OUTPUT_DIR`. Use `--stage test --release TEST_RELEASE.json` for the held day after its explicit release. SOURCE_DIR contains the split NPZ, its QC JSON, first-day-split.json, identities.json and held-day-membership.json. MODELS_DIR contains the two split prediction archives. Use `--metadata METADATA_DIR` if identity and membership JSONs live separately from the numerical reconstruction. TRAIN.npz may be the original accepted day-one archive or the authors’ exact array-level reproduction of all its original fields. The data-only JSON for held-day replay contains `{"status":"released","authorized":true,"split":"test"}` and is issued only after the development freeze. Test outputs are conditional on the fixed September 12 fits and the September 13 recordings from this animal.
