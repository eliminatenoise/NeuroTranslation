# Credits and reuse

Jorge German Perez Blanco and Javier Mauricio Anteliz Ortiz published the EEG-EMG-WristMotion Dataset, version 3, on Figshare under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. NeuroTranslation derived two signed angle histories and four bipolar forearm voltage amplitudes from cued right-wrist pointing, reused its seed-17 motion network and fitted an additive correction from the six designated training people. Keep the authors' attribution, identify subsequent changes and retain the CC BY 4.0 terms for the source, derived weights, scales and reported results. The accompanying records contain invented measurements.
