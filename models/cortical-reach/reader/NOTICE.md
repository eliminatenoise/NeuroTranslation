# Measurements and rights

Raeed H. Chowdhury, Joshua I. Glaser and Lee E. Miller recorded the macaque’s handle and arm movement and sensory cortical activity. The selected session is C_20170912_COactpas. The original study and its deposited data should both be credited. The Dryad data record declares CC0. This reader includes a numerical excerpt of the first trial in the previously assigned chronological test group, trial 499. Only the units designated by positive sorting codes enter the neural predictor. The graphics, fitted forecasts and source code are original NeuroTranslation materials, dedicated to the public domain under CC0 to the extent of the project’s rights. [Dataset](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.nk98sf7q7) · [Original paper](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.48198) · [Authors’ analysis code](https://github.com/raeedcho/s1-kinematics) · [CC0 terms](LICENSE)

Handle readings are encoder coordinates in centimetres. The arm markers were tracked, filtered, interpolated and aligned offline. Marker coordinates use a distinct shoulder-relative frame and metres. OpenSim joint angles and muscle lengths are estimates from a musculoskeletal model. This is a visual cue and mechanical perturbation task. No spoken instruction or natural-language transcript was recorded in these trial data.
