# Scoring methods

The chronological split preserves the rewarded original trials in macaque C’s 12 September 2017 recording. The first 324 originals form the training block. The next 108 form validation and the last 108 form the test block. The target is the recorded change in planar handle position over 100 ms.

## Primary and timing-specific questions

The primary contrast in cm equals the movement-plus-cue Transformer's Euclidean displacement error minus the joint movement/cue/spike Transformer's error. Predicting the endpoint by adding the origin to forecast displacement produces the same error. Positive values favor the joint model. On the *same recipient rows*, the timing contrast equals the joint model's error using a different earlier original's spike history minus its correctly paired error. Movement, cues, model weights, token order and cutoff remain the recipient's. The deterministic donor is found within the same partition on exact epoch, offset and cutoff-visible cue statuses and directions. Donor matching can discard early originals or sparsely represented directions. Name the latter a wrong-trial control. A difference cannot distinguish real-time timing effects from other trial-specific neural information.

Fix primary support to every eligible recipient with a valid earlier matched donor, including for all motion, neural, linear and physical control comparisons. No intersection can be selected by error; nondonor forecasts must be finite. Also present source support, exclusion counts, separate phase/direction results and, for sensitivity, the two main contrasts on recipient trials with both epochs represented. Separately tabulate the joint and movement/cue methods on all source-eligible rows, explicitly outside the donor comparison. Do not restrict fitting to donor-eligible rows. Report one model's error and component biases on the original scale in cm. Residual = predicted minus recorded. Window metrics are Euclidean distance, |x|, |y|, signed x and signed y. Show each seed; the main estimate averages the three *losses* per recipient, never the predictions or the animal count.

After restricting rows for a table, first average windows within each original and epoch. Then give each available epoch in that original equal weight and every represented original equal weight. For individual epochs or directions, recompute weights within the reported subset. For direction, use the epoch's own direction when visible at the forecast cutoff, otherwise 'unavailable'. Direction at the event boundary can be unobserved by construction. Report trial/epoch/window counts. Scores, paired effects and per-seed effects use identical rows and weights. Record point scores and trial/epoch tables in machine-readable form for independent reconstruction. No best-seed choice, subgroup selection, substitution of trials or post-validation model edits.

For each aggregate contrast and epoch, compute two-sided 95% percentile uncertainty intervals from 5,000 circular moving-block bootstrap draws of the entire original ordered split. Block length is eight consecutive rewarded originals, drawn with replacement with a uniform first index and wrapping; concatenate to the original split length and truncate the final block. Preserve unavailable originals in the ordered sequence and skip their undefined mean when aggregating each draw. Omit draws without support and record number supported. Use NumPy PCG64 seed 20261121 and reuse draw indices throughout a split. Freeze predictions and the donor mapping throughout (conditional on these fitted models, controls and recorded session). Estimate differences *within each paired draw*, averaging the three seed-specific losses within an original. Report seed range and individual paired effects separately. Intervals cannot estimate variation across animals, recording days, retraining populations, or alternate donors.

The primary answer is supported improvement if the pooled lower endpoint exceeds 0 cm, supported worsening if the upper endpoint falls below 0, and unresolved otherwise. To distinguish a trial-specific neural contribution, also require a positive lower endpoint for the prespecified timing control. If only one contrast passes, state exactly which comparison passed. No accuracy threshold or practically meaningful minimum was supplied. Seed-, epoch-, direction-, linear- and motion-control findings are descriptive.

## Fixed alternative models and controls

Fit affine ridge for all, movement+cue, neural+cue, neural-only, cue-only and movement-only. Each feature is one specified native token channel at one lag. Flatten the five tokens, center and standardize each feature using the same train-only observation weights as the neural fits (same original/epoch/window hierarchy). Divide by weighted population SD, using divisor 1 for SD below 1e-10. Fit unpenalized intercepts and minimize weighted mean squared residual plus 0.01 × sum of squared coefficients per output. Fix lambda here. No validation selection or test-time recalibration. Include joint ridge with the wrong trial, plus the following lower-dimensional neural controls to separate coarse firing level from structured history: joint with the five 40-ms population-total firing rates (events per second across units), and joint with 30 per-unit mean rates over the complete 200 ms (events/s/unit). Both include the original movement and cue inputs with the identical ridge objective. Store scaled coefficients, feature names, centers, divisors and the intercepts with original outcome units. These controls cannot establish spike timing mechanisms.

Report zero displacement; extrapolate endpoint velocity from the preceding 40, 100, and 200 ms using actual stored positions, predicting 100 ms as (p[k]−p[k−d]) × 10/d for d = 4,10,20. Never use archived velocity. Compare all eligible baselines on the primary matched support. Report Euclidean paired contrasts against combined Transformer for each, including wrong-trial and correctly timed joint ridge; compare joint ridge to its own motion/cue and wrong-trial controls. Include per-seed and per-epoch outputs and trial denominators. All single-session groups retain their chronological identity.
