The original treadmill recordings were released by Rateb Katmah, Aamna AlShehhi, Doua Kosaji, Nour Al-Rahmani, Muhammad Abdullah, Abdul Aziz Vaqar Hulleck and Kinda Khalaf (2026), *A multimodal gait dataset of brain activity, muscle activity, kinematics and ground forces in young adults*, version 1.0.0, PhysioNet, RRID:SCR_007345. The source is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. Retain this attribution and identify modifications when reusing derived material. [Dataset DOI](https://doi.org/10.13026/r0ea-7161) · [Source licence](https://physionet.org/files/multimodal-gait-dataset/1.0.0/LICENSE.txt) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

For the repository, see Pollard TJ et al. (2026), *PhysioNet as a global platform for biomedical research*, Nature Health. [PhysioNet platform paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44360-026-00096-z)

This package selects six published angle columns at one treadmill speed, builds time-ordered histories, fits new model weights and reports aggregate test errors. It includes an invented input for running the predictors. Obtain the participant recordings directly from PhysioNet. The Xsens manual is linked for its description of HD processing. [MVN user manual](https://www.xsens.com/hubfs/User_Manual_Xsens%202025.pdf)
