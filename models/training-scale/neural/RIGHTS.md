# Sources and reuse

Justin Shin and Shantanu Jadhav shared *Single Day W-Track Learning* in DANDI under CC BY 4.0. We used release 0.240511.0307, JS14/01 and ER1/01. We selected trials, grouped spike times into milliseconds, fitted two shared scoring rules and calculated summaries. Rat-derived results and fitted models, including mixed fits, carry the original attribution and CC BY 4.0 terms. JS14 asset 4b7c7959-04b3-4817-8f96-1af6f41888c1 and ER1 asset b6425692-0f32-4087-8412-aebcfc37cd96 identify the deposited NWB files. [DANDI release](https://doi.org/10.48324/dandi.000978/0.240511.0307) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

A. Loback, G. Tkačik, J. Prentice and colleagues deposited the salamander retinal recordings on Dryad under CC0. We used the repeated movie, checkerboard and moving bar experiments from archive version 1 and grouped their original 10 kHz samples into milliseconds. [Dryad data](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.1f1rc) · [Preserved source archive](https://zenodo.org/records/4976473) · [Methods](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1005148)

Shein-Idelson, Becker and Leberstein published *View invariant representations in ancestral cortex*. Their Dryad archive carries a CC0 dedication. We used cluster IDs, original event times and visual stimulus onsets in the authors’ figure material. [Dryad data](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.k98sf7mm6) · [Versioned figure file](https://github.com/EvolutionaryNeuralCodingLab/SpatiallySelectiveViewInvariance/blob/b421ce0a62140a812e964cb027b97f979e98f049/spikes.mat)

The original study code is dedicated to the public domain under CC0. The reference reader, original coefficients and verified ER1 byte-range extractor come from the Next Neuron Prediction study. Each source keeps its own terms.
