# Forecast shortening in the tracking plane

Question. How much will the image-tracked bundle of muscle fibres in the shin shorten or lengthen over ten further tracking steps? Does the bundle's earlier angle relative to the reference tissue improve a forecast based on its recent length? Study only the original seated dorsiflexion task. A01 trial 1 supplied the pre-existing intake; no new development data were inspected for the choices below.

Data and support. Use the Zhang et al. AnkleImage S-BIAD1482 Task 3 tracking tables from trials 1 and 2 of A01–A04 for training and separate four-person assessment A05–A08 after both families' prediction code, source selection, scoring and fitted weights are sealed. A09 stays reserved. Use original paired tracking knots, not the denser interpolated displays, EMG, ankle angle or the AVI display clock. Prefer source-qualified length (mm) and original signed pennation (degrees) with corresponding rows from the same tracking sequence. A and B are tracker outputs under the original source definitions. Do not substitute assumed defaults if the physical variables are unverified. A partial or missing person makes the planned four-person result incomplete. Report only qualified descriptive results alongside any unmet source requirements, without substitution.

For each trial use zero-based cutoffs 20,30,40,..., extending only where row i+10 exists. Require a continuous qualified tracking segment across knots i−20 to i+10, finite length and angle over the predictor and finite length at the endpoint; check native row order. No interpolation or padded targets. Forecast signed ΔL=L[i+10]−L[i], in mm. Positive means a longer image-plane tracking estimate. Inputs end at i; the measurement itself may involve offline processing or smoothing. Endogenous tracking estimates may share measurement or processing error. No inference about causal nerve drive, true three-dimensional muscle force, a clinical effect, or precise EMG timing.

Models. Compare a zero change, repeating the previous ten-step change, training-only weighted median signed change, length-only fitted linear and pretrained encoder, full geometry-aware linear and pretrained encoder, and a full fixed random encoder (same init seed 20261121, same trained readout size). Also compare a deterministic two-dimensional constant-separation model. Let x=L cos(theta), h=L sin(theta) with theta in radians. Extrapolate x by its last ten-step change while holding h at its cutoff value, then predict hypot(2x[i]−x[i−10],h[i])−L[i]. This is an idealized straight-bundle projection; neither a measured anatomical thickness nor a force law. Keep this result independent of fitting.

Use four chronological bins of five tracking gaps (six observations per bin, shared endpoints). Six quantities per bin: final length; last−first length; mean length residual from the straight line between endpoints (including endpoints); final angle; last−first angle; last−first longitudinal projection x. Standardize with the training-only weighted bin mean and standard deviation; floor tiny scale (<1e−8) to one. Withhold angle and projection by setting their three standardized features to zero in both length-only methods. Retain the previous ten-step length change as a direct feature in every learned model. Flatten the standardized tokens and append the anchor. Encoder models also append the last token embedding from the original 24-unit shared movement encoder (projection, positions, attention, MLP and normalization all frozen). Compare a fixed, untrained same-shaped encoder against the pretrained full fit; no pretrained superiority presumed. Standardize the final features on training only. Fit weighted ridge with penalty 0.05, unpenalized intercept, to ΔL minus the recent ten-step change; cap predicted shortening at minus the current length so the future length is nonnegative. Keep paths with and without angles matched. For a swapped prediction retain the trained full model and all recipient lengths but replace its complete angle history with that of the other trial of the same participant, at proportionate chronological rank floor(k*n_other/n_recipient); recompute all mixed projection features. No shared source observation may appear across a donor and recipient pair. Without a qualifying donor file, report the unavailable comparison.

Hold out each development person for one fold, then refit once on all four for independent assessment. Give equal weight to each person and within person to each trial. Report signed mean error, MAE, RMSE in mm, paired person-level gains (control MAE minus full MAE), number improving jointly and exact two-sided sign test (drop ties). Do not estimate population-level efficacy from four people. A practical paired-angle result in a complete four-person stage requires >=5% lower full macro MAE versus both the length-only pretrained fit and the same full model with wrong-trial angle, plus a joint improvement for at least three of four people. Report the full-vs-best-simple result (zero, recent, training median, geometry and linear length) and full pretrained vs linear full/random full separately. No adjustment of methods, stopping rules, cohort or thresholds after scores.
