Stand and sit force forecast

1. Extract the ZIP and keep the files together.
2. Open the file for your computer.
   Linux   Open on Linux.sh
   macOS   Open on macOS.command
   Windows Open on Windows.cmd
   You can also open index.html in a desktop browser. The preview runs offline.
3. Choose an invented force history. Read the forecasts for each floor
   plate and their combined upward impulse over the next 100 milliseconds.

The input is in newtons (N). The impulse forecast is in newton-seconds (N·s).
README.md gives the recorded test results. METHODS.md explains the three
forecast rules and how the test was scored.
