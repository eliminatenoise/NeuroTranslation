# Recording source and licence

The Utah comparison uses the stand-up and sit-down dataset by Grace Hunt, K. Bo Foreman, Tommaso Lenzi, Lukas Gabert and Colby Hansen (2024). [Figshare dataset, version 2](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.26282872.v2)

The source dataset is licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The comparison uses averages of upward force from two floor plates and reports aggregate forecast errors. The offline examples use invented force readings.

## Leeds chair trials

The Leeds replay uses the University of Leeds dataset by David Lunn, Enrico de Pieri, Graham Chapman, Stephen Ferguson and Anthony Redmond (2025). The original data are available under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). [University of Leeds dataset](https://doi.org/10.5518/1701)

David E. Lunn, Enrico De Pieri, Graham J. Chapman, Morten E. Lund, Stephen J. Ferguson and Anthony C. Redmond describe the acquisition and processing in their 2026 methods article. The replay uses the released vertical force to calculate averages and reports aggregate forecast errors. [Scientific Data article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-026-06925-w)

The project’s contributions to the code, fitted weights and documentation are dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
