# Sources and rights

The original inference and training code and the fictional example in this folder are dedicated under CC0 1.0 Universal. Our contributions to both trained parameter files and the evaluation results are offered under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. Attribute them to the Brain2Body programme, identify which fit was used and describe any changes. Source acquisition terms and credits remain applicable. [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

**Mammal weights.** The two learned heads in `mammal-weights.json` fit only the five-mouse open-field and four-gerbil tracker exports. Retain the accompanying SLEAP Clear BSD notice. Cite the SLEAP paper and the two recording teams listed below. [Pereira et al., Nature Methods (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-022-01426-1) · [Dataset licensing statement from a SLEAP collaborator](https://github.com/talmolab/sleap/discussions/2906#discussioncomment-19503747)

**Mixed weights.** The two heads in `mixed-weights.json` also fit the 32-point fly recording and worm records 1005864 and 1011834. Retain the SLEAP notice, the CC BY 4.0 notice and attribution for those recordings. The trained files contain numeric network parameters. Inputs were converted into normalized local kinematics and projected geometry and used for supervised prediction. [N2 worm recording](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.1005864) · [Long-body worm recording](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.1011834)

**Evaluation.** The additional acquisitions below supplied held-out optical positions for measuring forecast error. The locust comparison uses the GIN movement sample export and the original recording. [Genuine source archive](https://doi.org/10.17617/3.7F5MGE)
