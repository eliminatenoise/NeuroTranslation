What can stay the same across limbs?

The rule compares each muscle’s recent pattern with its own earlier amplitude. It learns combinations of past movement vectors. A muscle with a larger voltage scale should get a larger predicted voltage, and turning the coordinate frame should turn the movement forecast. Neither operation adds a new observation. The device used for the arm recordings has no verified conversion from its exported amplitude to microvolts.

The two experiments measured different people doing different tasks. Missing or unreliable muscle channels are omitted. A shared formula can be tested against separate fits on those two sets of recordings. Its error cannot establish that one limb has learned a skill from the other.
