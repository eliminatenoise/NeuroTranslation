# Timing qualification before fitting

The original A01 files include a regular stored time vector. A02's raw files contain aligned EMG, load-cell and trigger columns without a time vector. The authors report simultaneous acquisition at a nominal 1 kHz and their Task 1 code makes a regular time vector from the indices (even the stored A01 vector exactly matches those labels). The model's numerical endpoint remains the change exactly 100 acquired row positions after the cutoff. For files without timestamps, row positions establish a nominal 100 ms span only; gaps, absolute latency and hardware timing are unverified. A generated grid is never evidence of measured continuity. Keep exported and reconstructed clocks distinct, and leave a physical elapsed-time assertion unanswered wherever the necessary evidence is absent. Apply the native-unit, participant/task/trial separation and frozen improvement criteria to the row-based experiment. No new participant/feature/threshold or event-specific fitted choices. Source setting angle5 is the named acquisition setting, distinct from angle_5; do not assume its anatomical direction.
