# What does the plate under the right foot record after heel strike?

This separate study asks whether eight electrical signals from the right leg improve an estimate of later vertical load on the platform that caught the right foot. It should be interpreted alongside the whole-trial model for the sum of both platforms, which is a different outcome. Training-only source checks established the original contact coding and right-first-strike location. Both this design and its comparisons are fixed before inspecting the ten assessment people's force or EMG values.

Use the same v8 C2 roster of 30 fit, 10 assessment and 10 unopened reserve people, three selected repeats, as the two-plate study. Admit only original Supplementary Table 2 records coded 12 or 21, marking two available distinct platforms. Use exactly one source Right Foot Strike1 per trial, convert the event seconds to the nearest 100 Hz point index on the verified time origin. At the strike require both original right heel and second-metatarsal markers to be finite, have a nonnegative stored residual, and project inside or on the assigned platform rectangle. No force threshold, future measurement or toe-off event determines the input cutoffs. Exclude unavailable cells, rather than assigning a different foot. Report trial and person coverage and every exclusion reason. These rules describe a qualified subset. An exported platform value is not the total force borne by the person elsewhere on the walkway.

Try cutoffs 14, 24, 34 and 44 stored point frames after the strike. Require 24 frames of history through the cutoff and the full 15-sample target block ten frames later within the recording. The output is the mean upward laboratory-coordinate vertical force of the assigned platform at that block, in N. Include zero values, require finite target and a nonempty force circuit, and verify the flat source orientation and published sign. Do not mass-normalize. Source signals are event-cropped, partly interpolated, filtered in both time directions, and the archived EMG was centered across the trial. The recorded event time is supplied. No real-time acquisition or physiological lead time is established.

All main methods know elapsed time since strike. Use four disjoint chronological 60 ms bins across the last 24 optical frames. Each motion token uses the right heel and second metatarsal XYZ in metres relative to the assigned plate centre at the last point frame of its bin. Require all six such coordinates valid in each token. Each EMG token uses the RMS in microvolts for each of the eight previously specified right-leg channels over the corresponding 90 analog samples, followed by its 8 availability flags. Documented unavailable channels and windows with any invalid electrical sample use zero with availability flag zero. Transform RMS as log1p(microvolts). The raw marker input is flattened 4×6 geometry plus a four-category indicator of elapsed cutoff. The combined input appends 4×8 logged amplitudes and 4×8 availability flags in that order.

Fit a four-cell elapsed-time table, raw marker ridge, raw combined ridge, and frozen shared-encoder heads on the marker and combined inputs. The shared encoder gets the four six-dimensional marker tokens, z-scored using training-only feature means and standard deviations pooled over bins. It uses the existing spatial input projection, shared block, position and final normalization; its final hidden state is appended to the corresponding raw input (only). Also fit one combined scrambled control: independently permute values within every sorted tensor used by the original encoder with NumPy default_rng(71863). No other original model weights change. Per-person total weight is equal, then per trial equal within person, then per valid cutoff equal. Weighted squared loss on N; standardized features (SD <1e-8 replaced by 1), unpenalized intercept and ridge lambda 0.05 against mean squared loss. Fix weights on the train group once. For the phase table, average the training participants’ available per-trial values for each of the four elapsed positions. Unseen or empty cells use the overall weighted training mean.

Assess absolute N error within trial then person then equal test people. Show every comparable person's aggregate error and absolute coverage. On a separate matched subset, replace the combined model's electrical tokens with those from the next eligible repeat of the same test person at exactly the same elapsed offset, cyclically, skipping the current repeat; report unavailable rows and people. This is an offline control, never a deployable forecast. Compare holding the current measured platform force on valid earlier measurements, on exactly common rows. Compare a correctly aligned shared combined head with shared marker only, raw combined ridge, the elapsed-time table and the wrong-repeat control. Require lower aggregate MAE and strict wins in more than half of comparable test people against all four for an incremental claim, plus a negative upper bound on the paired person-resampled 95% interval against shared marker and wrong-repeat. Require frozen shared combined to beat scrambled combined on mean error and a majority for any frozen-representation claim. Log all outcomes regardless. Bootstrap 10,000 whole-participant resamples with seed 71864, descriptive given small numbers of walkers. No analysis of the temporal reserve of the other Stanford project or new human intervention is involved.
