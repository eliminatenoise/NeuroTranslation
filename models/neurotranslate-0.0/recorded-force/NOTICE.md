# Sources and licence

The [CC0 dedication](LICENSE) covers the new project-authored code, text, invented example, newly fitted input and output weights, and new aggregate scale values. The two random-start checkpoints use CC0. The `released-core.npz` combined checkpoint carries the unchanged original joint-alpha attention weights under [CC BY 4.0](licenses/CC-BY-4.0.txt). Retain the original attribution when redistributing that checkpoint. The demo needs a separately installed copy of Python and NumPy. [Python licence](https://docs.python.org/3/license.html) · [NumPy licence](https://numpy.org/doc/stable/license.html)

Tamaya Van Criekinge, Wim Saeys, Steven Truijen, Luc Vereeck, Lizeth H Sloot and Ann Hallemans collected the source gait recordings. This fit uses only a small eligible part of the healthy walking archive. It turns voltages into 20 ms root-mean-square summaries and recorded plate force into 20 ms means. Future force samples supply the new-load labels and signed impulse. [CC0 Figshare archive](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.24192480.v1) · [Scientific Data article, CC BY 4.0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-023-02767-y)

The released-core checkpoint also carries the original NeuroTranslate 0.0 shared attention weights. The earlier work transformed motion, neural activity and muscle and scalp recordings into fixed histories and task-specific targets. Its source credits include these recordings and their original licences. [Original model and preparation](https://github.com/eliminatenoise/NeuroTranslation/tree/main/models/neurotranslate-0.0/joint_alpha)
