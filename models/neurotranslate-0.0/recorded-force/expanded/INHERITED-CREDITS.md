# Source credits for the starting attention core

Both force-plate checkpoints start from the shared attention core of the NeuroTranslate 0.0 joint alpha release. The frozen version preserves its numerical values. The trainable version updates them using the walking force task. The original recordings remain with their publishers. Retain the applicable credits and terms and describe changes when redistributing the derived checkpoints. [Original release credits](https://github.com/eliminatenoise/NeuroTranslation/blob/8d79659461244952789d074c4f50382a4bf43060/models/neurotranslate-0.0/joint_alpha/NOTICE.md)

- Raeed Chowdhury, Joshua Glaser and Lee Miller recorded macaque arm movements with cortical activity. The original model preparation aligned movement and grouped spikes by recording contact. [Dryad release, CC0](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.nk98sf7q7)
- Steven Peterson and Daniel Ferris recorded human balance. The model preparation aligned scalp and leg muscle voltages with a marker on the sacrum. [OpenNeuro ds003739 version 1.0.3, CC0](https://doi.org/10.18112/openneuro.ds003739.v1.0.3)
- Justin Shin and Shantanu Jadhav recorded rats on a W-shaped track. The model preparation binned spike activity and formed histories for separately sorted units. [DANDI 000978 version 0.240511.0307](https://doi.org/10.48324/dandi.000978/0.240511.0307) · [CC BY 4.0](licenses/CC-BY-4.0.txt)
- Adam Atanas, Jungsoo Kim and colleagues recorded fluorescence in adult worms. Martin Haesemeyer and Kaarthik Balakrishnan recorded fluorescence in larval zebrafish. The model preparation used four earlier measurements to forecast the next reading. [Worm recordings](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19388374) · [CC BY 3.0 US](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/) · [Zebrafish recordings](https://doi.org/10.48324/dandi.000235/0.230316.1600) · [CC BY 4.0](licenses/CC-BY-4.0.txt)
- The SLEAP collection supplied tracked animal positions, including open-field mouse recordings from John D’Uva, Mikhail Kislin and Samuel S.-H. Wang, and gerbil recordings from Catalin Mitelut, Marielisa Diez Castro and Dan H. Sanes. The original comparison also used held tracks from the SLEAP collection and from Angela Albi, Daniele Carlesso, and Avelino Javer and their colleagues. The original preparation expressed displacement relative to visible body size.
