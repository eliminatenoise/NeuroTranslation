# Source and reuse

**Recorded data.** Raeed H. Chowdhury, Joshua I. Glaser and Lee E. Miller released their processed reaching recordings under CC0 1.0. Rhesus macaque C moved a robot handle between targets in two workspaces. The data excerpts include somatosensory cortical spikes, tracked arm coordinates, encoder positions and cue times. The source also supplies OpenSim estimates of muscle–tendon path lengths. The researchers produced the recordings and those estimates. The forecasts and transfer comparisons here were fitted separately. [Dryad data and licence](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.nk98sf7q7) · [Chowdhury, Glaser & Miller, eLife 9, e48198 (2020)](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.48198)

Each MATLAB member lives under `reaching_experiments/` in the original archive. [Official source ZIP](https://zenodo.org/records/5010943/files/s1-kinematics.zip)
