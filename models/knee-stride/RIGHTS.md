# Source and reuse

Rateb Katmah, Aamna AlShehhi, Doua Kosaji, Nour Al-Rahmani, Muhammad Abdullah, Abdul Aziz Vaqar Hulleck and Kinda Khalaf released *A multimodal gait dataset of brain activity, muscle activity, kinematics and ground forces in young adults*, version 1.0.0, through PhysioNet in 2026. PhysioNet's research resource identifier is RRID:SCR_007345. The release uses Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. Retain the attribution, link the licence and identify changes when using derived material. [Dataset DOI](https://doi.org/10.13026/r0ea-7161) · [Release licence](https://physionet.org/files/multimodal-gait-dataset/1.0.0/LICENSE.txt) · [CC BY 4.0 terms](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

PhysioNet asks users to cite Pollard TJ et al. (2026), *PhysioNet as a global platform for biomedical research*, Nature Health. [Platform paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44360-026-00096-z)

This study selects the 0.5 m/s treadmill exports, aligns their existing frame numbers, compares the two stored knee angles with an earlier stride, fits two corrections and reports errors by participant. The package distributes code, fitted parameters, source checksums, derived scores and an invented example. Download the trial recordings from the source. [Official dataset](https://physionet.org/content/multimodal-gait-dataset/1.0.0/)

The project dedicates its original code, model-side contributions, prose and invented example to the public domain under CC0 to the extent of its rights. Source-derived and third-party material retain their applicable terms. [CC0 dedication](LICENSE)
