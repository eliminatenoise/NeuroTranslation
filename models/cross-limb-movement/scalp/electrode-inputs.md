# Scalp inputs for the wrist and balance forecasts

The primary input uses 20 consecutive intervals over the 625 ms history. At each of three sites it carries the signed voltage mean, voltage root mean square and a binary observation flag. In order, the WAY sites are C3, Cz and C4. Their BioSemi nominal counterparts are D19, A1 and B22. Frontopolar comparisons use Fp1 and Fp2 (C29 and C16 in balance). The balance eye comparison uses right horizontal, left horizontal and left vertical EOG. The balance neck comparison uses left and right neck EMG. Every branch has three slots. A two-site branch leaves its last slot masked. A WAY row has no measurements in either dedicated control branch.

Join the frozen development archives by every recorded row key. Read the cohort's row keys only. Require the paired archive to carry previously validated channel labels, per-site flags, reason bits and the exact past-bin sample geometry. Keep its values in source units and preserve the ZERO cache convention for masked sites. Use strictly past-through-cutoff stored samples. WAY ends at cutoff minus 2 ms after dropping 2 ms at both edges of every 31.25 ms interval. Balance includes its cutoff sample. Validate source ordinals and cutoffs. Prior movement eligibility includes checks of later measurements. Balance preprocessing and channel rejection used the archived recording. Electrode mixtures, source references, detection delays, processing support and inter-system uncertainty constrain any real-time or anatomical inference.

Estimate each mean and population standard deviation over the available training rows and all 20 bins within study, named site and feature, accumulating in float64. The split must contain whole people. Neither identity, clocks, grouping fields, observed masks from assessment people nor their measurements enter the scale fit. For zero observations record mean zero, scale one, count zero. For an observed constant feature record its mean and scale one. A zero-training-count site cannot accept an observed input at evaluation. Use the same scale in every branch consuming a given site. Standardized missing values are zero. The matched reference sets both standardized amplitudes to zero and carries the actual observation flags unchanged. No clipping, per-person recentering, target values or future bins. The network receives float32 triples in slot-major order.

The wrong-history comparison replaces only the three central histories. Form strata within study, person, native session, task/series, rotation direction, ordinal third and all three central availability bits. Use a different original event from earlier in that same stratum. Sort by absolute series/session cutoff tick, breaking ties by original ordinal. For each target choose the most recent eligible earlier donor still unused as a donor for this comparison. Require an observed central site and strictly disjoint whole-event envelopes, including both history and outcome. A first event and any other unmatched row get donor index -1 and cannot enter a wrong-history comparison. Donors are never taken from future events. Whole-person folds keep each pair on one side of evaluation. No cross-study or across-task substitution, target inspection, retries or nearest-condition relaxation.

For WAY, the frozen cohort verifies that each series cutoff time equals the trial start time plus the local cue time plus 400 ms. Use the actual 500 Hz series clock rather than the repeated trial-local time. Verify the series tick is integral within 1e-7 tick. Protect all directly accessed kinematic and muscle target/history support [-313,+63] and one extra tick at either edge for the published synchronization uncertainty. The donor's inclusive [cutoff-314,cutoff+64] must end strictly before the target's corresponding envelope begins. Balance uses the continuous archived 256 Hz session clock. Its directly checked kinematic margin extends from -167 through +40 ticks. A 32-sample flat-run flag can depend on 31 samples beyond either edge. Use [-198,+71] inclusive. These local margins do not bound the support of whole-record preprocessing, source channel rejection or physical synchronization error. They verify nonoverlap on the recorded clock.

All eligible rows enter the primary central/reference comparison. For each alternative, compare paired predictions on the exact shared support of the methods involved, reporting event and person denominators. Dedicated eye and neck comparisons are confined to balance. Matching equalizes the site flags for each correct and wrong row. Branches using different physical sites can have different masks even on the same event. No previous assessment or reserve participant is admitted.
