# The people who made the measurements

Matthew D. Luciw, Ewa Jarocka and Benoni B. Edin recorded the grasp-and-lift trials in WAY-EEG-GAL. The Figshare participant deposits carry a CC0 dedication. The associated data paper credits its data and scripts under CC BY 4.0. The European Commission supported the original recordings through the WAY project (FP7-ICT-288551). This study uses the authors' channel names, reported faults and wrist marker. [WAY collection](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.c.988376.v2) · [WAY data paper and supplements](https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2014.47)

Steven M. Peterson and Daniel P. Ferris recorded leg surface electromyography and sacrum motion while the participant stood or walked during a rotation of the visual scene. Their OpenNeuro release carries a CC0 dedication. This analysis uses version 1.0.3. [Balance dataset](https://doi.org/10.18112/openneuro.ds003739.v1.0.3) · [Data and methods](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2021.107635)

The examples distributed with this model are mathematical signals. The weights and group scores come from the two published studies. NeuroTranslation dedicates its original code and materials in this package to the public domain to the extent of its rights. [CC0 1.0 Universal](LICENSE)
