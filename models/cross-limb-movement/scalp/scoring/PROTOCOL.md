# Does scalp history improve the next eighth of a second?

Compare the same small transformer trained with past central scalp measurements and trained with those values replaced by the fitting fold's study-specific references. Both receive the same availability flags, movement and muscle histories, task information, native scales, person folds, training seeds, batch orders, optimizer budget and architecture. The intended contrast is measured values minus reference values. A negative difference favors measured values.

## Outcomes and decision

For each event, compute the mean absolute error in future root-mean-square surface muscle amplitude across available named muscles. Report balance in microvolts and WAY in the exported amplitude units. Compute mean Euclidean distance in millimetres from the measured marker path at the 32 future samples spaced 1/256 s apart, through 125 ms. The balance marker is on the sacrum. WAY uses the right wrist. Loss precedes all averaging, including averaging across seeds. Within each person give equal weight to each of the four balance posture × rotation-direction cells or each observed WAY series and equal weight to events in a cell. Average each person's loss equally across the three seeds and each study's losses equally across its participants. Keep studies separate.

For each primary outcome in each study, pair central and reference losses within the same person, seed and event support. Resample whole-person vectors of seed-averaged differences using 5,000 draws with replacement of the observed number of people. Initialize NumPy default_rng(1641) separately per study and draw integer indices with shape (5000, n_people). Use the same draws across outcomes. Report the paired mean, the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles, and the one-sided 98.75th percentile using linear quantiles. A primary advantage requires **all four** mean differences and **all four** one-sided limits strictly below zero. Zero fails. Familywise alpha is .05 for the four directional upper limits. Report every result and all three seed-specific study means, their range and sample standard deviation. The intervals describe variation across held development participants conditional on these fitted models. Overlapping training folds, only six wrist participants and a single development cohort limit inference. Seed variation adds no independent participants.

## Controls on equal support

For every control, take the intersection of its eligible rows with those of the measured central model before reducing either member. Retain observed cells with the original equal-cell weighting and report both matched score levels, their paired differences, events, people, and the number of excluded events and people with no eligible event. A zero-support control has counts only. Report ordinary 95% paired intervals on the two error differences for descriptive comparisons. The 98.75% threshold selects only on the primary central-versus-reference comparison. No secondary endpoint, site, subset, control or seed can substitute for it.

* Wrong trial uses the central model trained on aligned measurements. At inference, keep the target movement, muscle and task history and availability. Substitute an eligible nonoverlapping history from another trial of the same participant, study, session/series, direction and block with the same central-site mask. The input preparation fixes the donor without using target outcomes. Only rows with eligible donors count. This measures dependence on alignment in a fixed fitted model. It does not measure how well an independently trained wrong-trial predictor could perform.
* Fit separate frontal, eye and neck input branches under the joint training design. Frontal uses two measured positions, Fp1/Fp2 (C29/C16 in balance). Eye and neck use the independent balance recording channels. WAY has frontal scalp only as an artifact comparison. It has no measured independent eye or neck channels. Each branch requires at least one measured channel to contribute to its reported error.
* Reuse the verified original shared muscle/movement transformer and both per-study specialists. Exposure matching uses the original target-study training exposure. Total matching gives the specialist the full optimizer-step budget in its own study. Scores retain the same fold, seed, rows and units. These branches quantify the comparison to the original movement/muscle design and depend on their historical architectures.
* Muscle persistence independently predicts future amplitude from the previous 125-ms muscle RMS. Stationary position predicts zero future displacement. For exact constant velocities use the preceding 125-ms trajectory. For 31.25 ms subtract index 23 from index 31 and divide by 0.03125 s. For 125 ms divide its last point, relative to its start, by 0.125 s. Multiply each velocity by the future offsets 1..32 divided by 256. Also retain the original age-corrected 1- and 4-patch-knot velocity controls to identify differences in past sampling. Each motion rule pairs with the same independent muscle persistence prediction. Never use future samples to construct a baseline.

## Descriptive patterns

Report endpoint distance at 125 ms, per-axis absolute and signed whole-path error in source axis order (balance ML, SI, AP and WAY X, Y, Z), and named muscle errors with observed-channel counts. Quantify the primary paired contrast by balance posture, posture × rotation and WAY series and fully observed versus masked muscle windows. Show central-minus-reference by each of the three central sites present and absent and by the number available, one, two or three. These overlapping subsets describe who had usable measurements. They do not isolate any electrode's contribution. Report site and control channel availability in events and distinct people overall and by condition. Report numbers of people with lower error and primary joint direction counts. Publish aggregate metrics only with at least two contributing people, suppress singleton-field scores, and omit any leave-one-out or other summary that recovers a single person's score in two-person cells. No participant IDs, event rows, raw recordings or per-person losses belong in the public output.

The original movement/muscle selection screened the future outcome window. The balance archive has already applied signal processing and retrospective channel screens. WAY's published synchronization error can reach 2 ms. This offline experiment tests conditional forecast information in these retained recordings. It cannot identify neural generators, motor commands, intention, causal preprocessing or actuation. A population claim needs a prospective independent test.
