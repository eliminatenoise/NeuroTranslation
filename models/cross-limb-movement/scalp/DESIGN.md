# Does a scalp history add to a muscle and movement forecast?

Two development archives supply 6,943 aligned events. The balance study measured a sacrum marker and eight leg muscles. The reach-and-lift study measured a wrist marker and the available channels among five arm and hand muscles. The models predict the next 125 ms of native surface muscle amplitude and 32 three-axis marker displacements in millimetres. Independent people are the unit of evaluation. Retrospective eligibility, offline processing, recording artifacts and the wrist system's timing uncertainty of up to 2 ms limit the interpretation. Each scalp site samples a mixture of electrical sources.

## Computation and handoff

Freeze implementation, score rules, metadata, training-only scales, input adapter and splits before any fit or evaluation. On training rows only, run identical 16-step central prefixes at CPU3, CPU1 and ROCm1; compare loss and native outputs at absolute tolerance 0.01 and record cold time, thread pools and memory. All scientific fits use the retained CPU3 route irrespective of prefix results. One process, one interop thread, no loader workers; NumPy BLAS/OMP/MKL pools each one thread. Torch owns kernel/workgroup shapes. Reuse the in-memory batch and explicit transfers. Checkpoint each finished fit, verify initial/final states, example counts and row hashes.
