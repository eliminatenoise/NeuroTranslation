Does earlier acceleration help?

Two comparisons decide the angular-velocity test. First, remove acceleration and train again. Then keep the full model and borrow acceleration from the closest earlier qualifying recording by the same person, hand and target at the same elapsed time. Each comparison uses the same eligible windows with a donor.

A vector error is the straight-line distance between the predicted and recorded three-axis means. Smaller values are better. We compute each training seed’s error before averaging, then give each recording equal weight within its participant and each participant equal weight. The interval comes from resampling whole participants 10,000 times. A negative difference favors the full temporal model. An interval spanning zero leaves the direction unresolved. This interval describes the average difference across participants, rather than the uncertainty in a single trace.

Where the readings come from

Tim Sziburis, Susanne Blex, Tobias Glasmachers and Ioannis Iossifidis recorded 31 people in November and December 2022. They published the Ruhr Hand Motion Catalog in 2025. This forecast uses 24 people who already contributed training recordings. Each hand carried the cylinder toward one of nine assigned visual targets. Within each hand and target, repetitions 3–6 trained the models, 7–8 served for validation and 9–10 supplied the held-out results. Both original quality flags had to pass.

The gyroscope measures angular velocity in radians per second. The accelerometer reports metres per second squared, including gravity. All three axes turn with the sensor on the cylinder. Its first exported sample starts the clock. The exact instant of the cue and the instrument’s processing delay remain unknown. A change to a reading has no observed physical outcome. Recovering a wrist movement or an optical path would require other measurements.

For each hand we show the earliest eligible recording and the earliest with a usable earlier match, ordered by source participant then native recording number. Every eligible cutoff in each chosen recording is available. The selection used recording order alone.
