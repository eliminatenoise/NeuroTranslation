# Sources and reuse

Tim Sziburis, Susanne Blex, Tobias Glasmachers and Ioannis Iossifidis created the *Ruhr Hand Motion Catalog of Human Center-Out Transport Trajectories Measured Redundantly in 3D Task-Space*. The experiments took place in November and December 2022. The data descriptor appeared in 2025. The original dataset and its numeric excerpts carry Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. [Original dataset](https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/J3QC8) · [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-05576-7) · [License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) · [Local license text](CC-BY-4.0.txt)

NeuroTranslation selected repetitions and valid windows from version 1 of the source archive, kept two measured three-axis channels, expressed times relative to each recording’s first inertial sample and averaged the ten future readings. The distributed excerpts use the original sensor coordinate frame and units, with no smoothing or gravity subtraction by this reader. NeuroTranslation trained the supplied temporal and linear models on the third through sixth repetitions. The example editor changes an in-memory copy of the earlier readings.
