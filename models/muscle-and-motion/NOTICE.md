# The recordings

Steven M. Peterson and Daniel P. Ferris recorded leg surface electromyography and optical marker positions during perturbed standing and walking. Their OpenNeuro release carries a CC0 dedication. This package uses version 1.0.3 and its visual-rotation sessions. The browser example was generated mathematically. The weights and group scores were derived from measured recordings. [OpenNeuro dataset](https://doi.org/10.18112/openneuro.ds003739.v1.0.3) · [Methods and data description](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2021.107635) · [Original balance study](https://doi.org/10.1523/ENEURO.0207-18.2018)
