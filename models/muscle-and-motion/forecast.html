One eighth of a second

A scene tilts. Eight electrodes pick up voltage in the lower legs, and a camera tracks a marker at the base of the spine. Give the model the last 160 samples. It forecasts the strength of each muscle’s electrical signal and the next 32 positions of the marker.

The example below is invented. Change the posture or rotation to see how the fitted model responds to the same signal history.
