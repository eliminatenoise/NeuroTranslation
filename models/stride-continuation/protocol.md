# Whole-body stride continuation protocol

Freeze this cohort, marker specification and method before inspecting numerical motion, fitting or assessment. Source groups, declared outcomes and options stay fixed. Use only the 31 source training trials, the previously inspected secondary groups, and the fresh confirmation groups listed in cohort.json. Never open reserve groups 08 and 141. Obtain fresh files after all learned and baseline fits and the evaluation code are sealed. Preserve acquisition failures and exclusions. Each fresh subject number has one action in this cohort, so action and subject number are confounded there. CMU permits one person to appear under more than one number.

## Forecast clock and coordinates

Accept only native point headers of 60 or 120 Hz, millimetres, and absent or +Z screen vertical. Select every third or sixth sample from the first native recorded frame to yield 20 Hz. Keep header first/last positions, original point-frame numbers and rates. No interpolation, filtering, rescaling or denoising. Use the fixed 18 source sites in schema.json. Grouped skin markers are arithmetic means. Check for finite observations and nonnegative residuals. Process only named unambiguous channels. Input spans −500 to 0 ms (11 frames). Predict +50 through +600 ms (12 frames). Schedule origins from 0.50 s after the first recorded frame, then every 0.10 s, including origins too close to the end for a complete outcome. No stride-event alignment, future course or lookup into adjacent trials. Input eligibility requires all 11 sampled site arrays finite and at least 40 mm between the last two waist sites in the horizontal plane. Log scheduled, past-eligible, observed-future and excluded counts separately. Require all 12 future sampled frames finite only for training/scoring. No future frame may change a past decision or model input.

At the final observed frame, horizontal right is the unit vector from left waist to right waist, up is lab +Z, forward is up cross right. Hold this basis through the forecast. Pelvis is the mean of the left and right waist at each instant. Input uses body sites relative to each frame's pelvis, and past pelvis relative to the final observed pelvis, in this basis. Learn changes relative to the last observed body pose and the extrapolated pelvis from the last 200 ms of pelvis travel. Preserve absolute translation for conversion back to lab coordinates. All methods project the two waist sites to have a common mean at the predicted pelvis, using a uniform shift to every site. Report site error relative to each frame's own pelvis and pelvis error in the original millimetres.

## Frozen fits and controls

Train a conditional Transformer and an otherwise identical history-only Transformer (both labels map to the walk index in the latter). Eleven 57-coordinate poses at scale 250 mm, cumulative origin as above, learned time and action embeddings, model dimension 48, four attention heads, two pre-normalized self-attention/feedforward blocks with ReLU, feedforward width 96. Only observed past tokens enter self-attention. Last encoded token predicts body and root residuals (scale 100 mm) at six even grid horizons. Linearly interpolate the odd horizons from zero residual at forecast origin. Use AdamW lr .001, weight decay .001, 850 batches of 48 sampled with replacement, uniform over groups then clips then windows. Fix model initialization 2511, sampling seed 34211, no dropout or parameter selection. Loss is mean squared body-coordinate error plus equally weighted mean squared root-coordinate error in common scaled units. Use final update, each fit exactly once beyond bounded matched resource trials.

Compare pelvis and body persistence; constant velocity for both estimated from the last 100 ms; linear ridge on all 11 poses (scaled as above, unpenalized intercept, weighted by group then clip then window, regularization .002 on mean objective), recovering 12 residual vectors; and action-independent nearest training continuations. For retrieval use the three distinct training recordings whose closest observed histories have least RMS discrepancy across the 11 scaled poses and 57 coordinates, inverse-distance weighted with a floor of .01 in scaled units. Apply their future residuals to the query's last body and extrapolated root. No test data enter fitting or retrieval galleries. Input sensitivity checks keep fitted conditional parameters fixed and (a) swap walk/run labels for all trials or (b) repeat the final observed pose throughout the entire history. Compare runtime perturbation with the separately fitted history-only control. Catalogue descriptors give action metadata; no speech, measured cue timing or natural-language comprehension is present.

## Reading the held-out results

Primary set is the fresh cohort. Secondary set is the three earlier assessment groups, analyzed separately. Primary endpoint is mean pelvis-relative 18-site Euclidean distance over 12 horizons. Also report the root curve, body and pelvis at 250/600 ms, each trial and group, failures and eligibility counts. Weight windows equally within a recording, recordings equally within a group, groups equally in each cohort. Publish all groups even if they contribute no score. The conditional model passes the body-error comparison only if its primary endpoint strictly beats persistence, constant velocity, ridge, retrieval and the history-only fit. Assess whether action helped using the fixed matched fit and swapped-input controls. No pooled independent-window confidence interval or participant-level extrapolation. A loss stays a loss. No tuning, fit changes, relabeling or chosen subgroup after evaluation.

## Resource qualification

One CPU process for each serial fit, one inter-op thread, no loader workers, BLAS/OpenMP initially one. Compare 1 vs 2 intra-op threads on identical first 32 updates and training samples before deployment. Retain the faster candidate only when loss and predictions agree within 0.001 scaled units; otherwise take one thread. CPU weights and NumPy inference must reproduce the PyTorch output within 0.01 mm on a training prefix. The CPU route uses framework-managed vector kernels. No GPU run or speed claim is required for this compact CPU deployment. Log hardware, pressure, versions and observed throughput. The public example uses an identified CMU training prefix under source terms. Original data remain optical marker observations. Skin markers, the derived pelvis and predicted future are separate from camera pixels, muscle voltage and clinical outcomes.
