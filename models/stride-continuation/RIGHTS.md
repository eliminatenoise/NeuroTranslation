# Source rights and credits

The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217.

Carnegie Mellon University’s Graphics Lab permits use of the motion data for research and inclusion in commercial products. Its FAQ permits copying, modification and redistribution. CMU prohibits direct resale of the data, including converted motion data. Those terms cover the measured portions of the two training examples. Each example identifies the recording, original C3D hash, exact sampled frames and source clock. [CMU terms](https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/) · [CMU FAQ](https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/faqs.php)

The project releases its original code, documentation, fitted weights and model-generated coordinates under CC0 1.0 Universal to the extent it holds rights in those contributions. Raw source recordings and third-party libraries retain their own terms. The package includes the two short measured excerpts and no original C3D files. [CC0 legal text](LICENSE)

The C3D reader is ezc3d. Training uses PyTorch and NumPy. Each library carries its own license. [ezc3d](https://github.com/pyomeca/ezc3d) · [PyTorch](https://pytorch.org/) · [NumPy](https://numpy.org/)
