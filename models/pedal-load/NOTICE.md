# Source and reuse

Qiang Zhang, Noor Hakam, Oluwasegun Akinniyi, Ashwin Iyer, Xuefeng Bao and Nitin Sharma released *AnkleImage* under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. This study selects two channels, two pedal settings and two trials at each setting for four people, fits forecasts and reports aggregate errors. The histories provided for interactive use are mathematical examples. Retrieve the original trials from the public deposit with the included extractor. [Dataset and accession](https://doi.org/10.6019/S-BIAD1482) · [Data licence](CC-BY-4.0.txt) · [Acquisition details and changes](source/RIGHTS.md)
