# Predicting the heel marker and upward plate load 100 ms later

The analysis was specified after a three-row development pilot and before the two assessments. Source and processing are Schreiber & Moissenet, Figshare 7734767.v8. Participant ordinals 1–30 and their first three C2 repeats are the only fitting source. The source reader fixes the row, contact and timing requirements. Every accepted pair is used. Historical participant ordinals 31–40 supply the assessment after fitting. Ordinals 41–50 remain untouched. In a small number of valid trials per subject, population and phase claims remain exploratory.

**Input.** Four ordered six-frame bins from the last 24 stored marker frames. In each bin take the last heel relative to the heel at the first frame of the 24, final forefoot minus heel, the heel and forefoot last minus first in the bin (each 3D), final heel height and annotated strike-relative cutoff offset in frames. Append log1p of each of eight right-leg EMG RMS values in µV from the source owner's four strictly cutoff-limited blocks. This produces 4 × 22 features. The phase covariate is a known elapsed time since annotated strike and presumes strike availability at inference. Absolute laboratory XY, participant ID, repeat, plate, future geometry, foot-off, force history and target values are unavailable to all primary methods. Source measurement filtering is bidirectional and event/trial processing retrospective. This is an archived-signal forecast on contact-eligible rows.

**Predictors.** Train a width-16 learned linear embedding, ordered learned position vectors, one 2-head self-attention residual block, learned layer normalization, two-layer 32-hidden tanh feed-forward residual block and learned layer normalization. Apply two linear readouts to the last output token for signed XYZ heel displacement (future minus own cutoff in mm, +Z up) and the mean of 15 future laboratory upward-force samples in N. All tokens end by the same prediction cutoff. The individual independent 3D and 1D specialists have identical full trunk capacity, initial parameters, input exposure per update, batch, seed, optimizer and search budget. One specialist pair has twice the trunk parameters/forward passes overall. Joint loss averages per-axis standardized motion MSE and standardized force MSE equally, with separate fold-local training means and standard deviations for all four outputs. Fit feature normalization per dimension over all four bins on training rows. Within every fit/evaluation, give each participant equal weight, then each accepted repeat equal weight, then each eligible row equal weight. Population scales use that hierarchy and a floor of 1e-6. Report separate nonzero shared gradients from each outcome and the norm of their weighted-sum residual, plus actual shared-trunk weight movement.

**Fixed tuning budget.** AdamW (lr .003, decay .01, default betas), full-batch float32, no dropout, seed 8347. Candidates 12, 36, 90 updates. Three outer folds assign each nominal fit ordinal (ordinal−1) modulo 3. To pick an epoch for each outer fold, sort eligible remaining people by training ordinal and use indices 0, 3, 6, ... as inner validation. Require ≥2 outer training people after the inner split, ≥1 outer evaluation person and ≥1 inner validation person. If any fold fails, stop with support and no evaluation. Refit on all remaining outer training people at the chosen epoch and score each outer evaluation person once. For final fit, choose among all three candidates by their mean person-weighted outer validation loss from predictions at each epoch on the complete outer training fit. For model selection use standardized native endpoint error divided by sqrt(sum training axis variances) and force MAE divided by training force SD; joint averages the two, specialists score their own outcome. Resolve ties in candidate order. All learned baselines use identical splits and hierarchy. Never choose a primary model from assessment results.

**Controls.** Full joint vs separately trained full specialists is the primary paired test. Train a marker-and-phase-only joint Transformer by zeroing the standardized eight EMG columns throughout. Compare jointly predictive weighted ridge regressions on flattened tokens with and without EMG, separately choosing each outcome's ridge coefficient from .01, .1, 1 for an intercept plus weighted mean-square loss. Include a three-nearest distinct training-people marker-history predictor using standardized marker-and-phase features and a simple arithmetic mean of the three targets (use available distinct people if fewer). Include training-only phase force profiles by weighted mean at exact offset (nearest available offset; ties lower), stationary displacement (zero), last 60-ms velocity × 100 ms, previous 100-ms displacement, and privileged past-force hold and linear trend as separate measurements. Include an initialized frozen random full encoder with ridge heads tuned as above. Wrong-repeat EMG is evaluation-only. Within each participant and exact offset, cyclically rotate entire EMG bin matrices by one in ascending eligible repeat among groups of ≥2, with no replacement/self match. Evaluate true, wrong, and no-EMG predictions on exactly that eligible subset; show donor coverage. Never fill unmatched groups with other people.

**Decision and uncertainty.** Primary error is Euclidean 3D heel endpoint error in mm and absolute upward-force error in N. Report both separately, and signed mean errors per XYZ coordinate and force. For a joint-over-specialists claim the joint must have lower mean on both outcomes AND the upper endpoints of both paired 95% bootstrap intervals for joint-minus-specialist must be below zero. Also identify whether it beats every equal-access simple comparator in its outcome; keep privileged force separate. To support incremental EMG on either outcome require negative upper intervals for full-minus-marker-only and full-minus-wrong (the latter on the identically matched subset); report both outcomes and no multiple-comparison correction. Assessment and nested out-of-fold calculations are separate. Calculate errors within each walk, then within each person, and give central 95% resampling intervals (5,000 draws with replacement of whole participants; seed 7103; no window-level independence). Interpret as descriptive with overlapping train folds and conditioning on eligible repeats. No physical uncertainty bound follows from stored target precision. The four fitted weight files and the NeuroTranslate movement readout were fingerprinted before assessment.
