# Attribution and reuse

Kai Joshua Miller deposited *A library of human electrocorticographic data and analyses* in the Stanford Digital Repository. Miller, Hermes, Honey, Hebb, Ramsey, Knight, Ojemann and Fetz describe the finger task in *Human Motor Cortical Activity Is Selectively Phase-Entrained on Underlying Rhythms* (2012). The Stanford version 11 catalogue licenses its material under Creative Commons Attribution–ShareAlike 4.0 International and requires respect for privacy and confidentiality. [Repository and use conditions](https://purl.stanford.edu/zk881ps0522.json) · [Library descriptor](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-019-0678-3) · [Original experiment](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1002655)

This adaptation selects the early portions of five development recordings, aligns contacts by anatomical area, computes two bands of electrical power and five glove-movement targets, and trains and tests held-person readouts. It provides study summaries, trained weights and numerical predictions for an invented input.
