# Scoring a reach and the later cortical counts

Twenty completed 10 ms bins precede each subsequent-go cutoff. The target movement comprises ten displacements from the final known pose. The final target lies 100 ms after that pose and 90 ms after the cutoff. The cortical outcome occupies the following [100, 200) ms. A row is one eligible go, and several rows can come from one original sequence. This study concerns one recording from one macaque.

## Point estimates and controls

Compute each seed's errors at the window level. Average within each original sequence first and then give each represented original equal weight. The reported three-seed mean averages these scores after loss computation. Raw coverage counts have explicit denominators and never serve as weights. Hand and elbow refer to the released optical Marker_1 and Pronation_Pt1 coordinates in metres, multiplied by 1,000 for mm. Handle position comes from the encoder in cm. No metric pools these coordinate systems. Endpoint miss is the final Euclidean residual norm. Trajectory error averages Euclidean distances over all ten horizons. Report each horizon, each signed final-axis residual (prediction minus measurement), and componentwise mean absolute error and per-window root mean square error. The observed-motion section uses actual displacement and its signed components.

The stationary forecast is zero displacement. Backward constant velocity uses the difference between the final two observed poses and projects it for one to ten further 10 ms steps. Each go-specific trajectory is the original-balanced mean of training trajectories for that ordinal. An unseen ordinal falls back to the unconditional train mean. Report the Transformer and ridge comparisons and their ablations. Full training-set motion fits are fit diagnostics. Separate forward-fold movement scores evaluate the genuinely held-out forecasts used as inputs to the count heads.

Score each sorted unit separately. The primary count loss sums the ten true observations and ten expected counts for each unit, computes Poisson deviance, and averages over the 23 sorted units. With y observed and m expected the deviance is 2[y log(y/m) − y + m]. Use the analytic zero limit for the log term, and floor m at 0.000001 for this computation. Floor totals after summing raw predictions. For bin-level deviance floor each bin separately. Absolute error and calibration use unmodified forecasts. Record total and bin-level mean absolute error, deviance, per-unit and per-horizon results, observed and predicted total counts by unit, raw observed zero counts and positive observations paired with exact zero baseline forecasts. All-zero units remain on the unit roster. The original-balanced delayed-count mean and ordinal-conditioned delayed-count mean are estimated separately for each bin and unit on supported training originals, excluding fold 0. An unseen ordinal uses the overall training estimate. Persistence repeats each unit's mean count in the final ten recorded history bins across all output bins. Concurrent counts appear only as descriptive coverage.

Forward folds exclude warmup-fold originals from all count arms, including the direct comparison. Neural forecast features use the same seed's motor model; the GLM uses the ridge motor forecast. All non-training forecasts use the train-only motor refit. An Oracle head that sees the measured future motion has privileged outcome information. Its comparison is descriptive. For the wrong-movement control, keep the true past context and apply the same full count head to movement predicted for the most recent strictly earlier distinct supported original in the same split, native block and go ordinal. Neither future tracking nor measured movement participates in selecting donors. The first eligible go of each ordinal in its block lacks a donor. ## Inference and support

Paired difference is full minus comparator. Bootstrap sorted original sequences, preserving all reaches, seeds, units and both arms within the same original. Keep every position in the predefined split roster even if it has no usable or paired row. For the primary interval, sample non-circular contiguous eight-position blocks with replacement. Use a uniform start in [0, n−8], concatenate enough blocks to span n positions and truncate the final block at n positions. Do this independently within each native recording block; the current source has one such block. An empty position carries no difference. Each draw averages the supported original differences at its selected positions, counting repeats. No reordering, gap removal or end-to-start wrapping. For sensitivity, draw n individual original positions with replacement and use the same missing-support rule. Both schemes use 5,000 draws with NumPy default_rng(20261125), reset once per scheme on each split, reusing the sampled positions across contrasts and seeds. Report linearly interpolated 2.5% and 97.5% endpoints and counts of defined replicates. A draw with zero support is undefined. With fewer than two supported originals return descriptive values only. A native roster block too short for eight slots has no moving-block interval.

Report each seed's signed comparisons and their mean; use the mean of seed-wise loss differences for the prespecified gates. For the motor gate on transfer workspace, the upper moving-block bound for full minus pose+cue must be below zero for hand and for elbow. For the count gate within each held workspace, both full minus direct and full minus wrong on common support must have upper bounds below zero. A cross-workspace demonstration requires both tests to pass, scored together under one frozen release. There is no pooled test. No threshold is adapted from the data. Handle, individual-unit intervals, GLM and other controls, privileged forecasts, ordinal and early/late comparisons are secondary diagnostics. Early and late divide each split's complete chronological original roster at its midpoint (odd extra entry in the earlier half). Every held result, including failure, remains visible.

The full source ledger retains attempted reaches and a zero-validation first-target design. Sufficient future tracking, reward and horizon availability define this retrospective cohort. The released trajectories were filtered and reconstructed, and native camera visibility is unknown. Task words describe observed controller events; hand trajectories cannot recover unrecorded target coordinates. No inference here establishes sensory causation, language decoding, peripheral neural activity or live control.
