# AnkleImage and reuse

Qiang Zhang, Noor Hakam, Oluwasegun Akinniyi, Ashwin Iyer, Xuefeng Bao and Nitin Sharma deposited the AnkleImage recordings under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. This analysis selects lengths and pennation angles reported by the original tracker for Task 3, fits coefficients and compares forecast errors. The example histories were invented for the reader. Obtain the original recordings from the authors’ archive using the included acquisition program.
