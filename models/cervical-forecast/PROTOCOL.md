# Can an early surface ENG channel help predict a later cervical electrode reading?

Prospective development protocol, fixed before reading any signal or label. The initial design called for median-nerve stimulation run 03 (see the catalog rule in ACQUISITION-CLARIFICATION.md), first 80 eligible chronological events each from sub-001, sub-002, sub-003 and sub-004. Four leave-one-person-out development folds. Each fold trains on exactly 240 events and predicts 80 events from the fourth person exactly once. The four development folds were fixed without tuning, checkpoint search or trial exclusion. Unexpected schema, nonfinite data, missing blocks or changed channel interpretation stop the fit.

**Forecast.** Predict the mean of cervical surface channel SC6 at [12,14) ms minus its mean at [-15,-10) ms relative to the event, in microvolts. Predictor windows are [-15,-10) and [5,8) ms, native 10 kHz, fifty and thirty samples respectively. At 8 ms all predictors are available from these recordings; the forecast window starts 4 ms later. Use the unfiltered source windows in microvolts. Acquisition filters are inherited. No symmetric postprocessing, fitted per-person test transforms, data-dependent rejection, future stimulus information or late-window input.

**Tokens and channel contract.** Average successive groups of five samples (0.5 ms) within each window. Concatenate ten baseline tokens then six early tokens. Input channel order is Biceps (axillary surface ENG per acquisition), SC6, Thumb (surface muscle channel), CP4 (scalp), ECG (acquisition-verified equivalent allowed). Non-ENG predictors use the latter four channels in both windows. Both candidate Transformers omit baseline Biceps entirely. Only the full candidate receives its early Biceps values. Fit a separate mean and population SD per active channel and window from training rows/times; replace a scale below 1e-8 µV with 1. Standardize target with training mean/SD. Mask unavailable inputs to exact zero after scaling and keep architecture, initial weights, order, optimizer and event-time covariate identical. Supply zero-based index / 79 as an explicit event-order covariate on every token. Sixteen learned positions distinguish the separated windows, with no interpolation across the gap.

**Comparators.** Training event mean; training ridge on event order; mechanical baseline predicting training mean raw late SC6 minus observed baseline SC6; pre-event-only ridge using the first ten tokens of the four non-ENG channels plus order; matched ridge regressions without/with early Biceps on flattened signal tokens plus order. Use an unpenalized intercept and solve sum of squared standardized residuals + 32 times squared coefficients. For the matched Transformer use input width 6, learned projection to 16, one pre-normalized, two-head scaled dot-product self-attention block, residual feedforward 16→32→16 GELU, final layer norm, mean across tokens, affine scalar output. No dropout. Seed 1701, AdamW (learning rate 0.003, weight decay 0.01, default betas), 100 full-batch updates with squared standardized error. No early stopping. Fit both versions and export their folds' predictions and the all-development versions for independent reproduction.

**Matched negative control.** For each person rotate the early Biceps event axis by +37 modulo 80. Keep each trace whole and retain original target, all other channels and event index. Train and score an otherwise identical ridge and Transformer on these mismatched pairs; each training/test person is deranged separately. This is an offline pairing diagnostic, never an online predictor, a null distribution or a p-value.

**Evaluation.** Primary measure is the unweighted mean of four person-specific MAEs (µV). Positive paired gain = MAE comparator minus MAE full candidate. Show all four paired differences, mean and range, all per-person MAE/RMSE and absolute prediction errors. Include the number of persons improved and exact two-sided participant-level sign-test p for the primary non-ENG vs ENG contrast (drop ties). Even unanimous signs among four give p=0.125. A development signal consistent with incremental ENG information requires strictly positive mean Transformer gain against BOTH matched non-ENG and deranged-pair Transformers and improvement on both contrasts in at least three of four people. Also report against linear, pre-event and mechanical comparators separately. No claim of outperforming them if the corresponding contrast fails. The primary question concerns prediction of this electrode reading only. Baseline subtraction couples predictor and outcome mathematically; the mechanical comparator makes this visible. Stimulus, volume conduction, motion, cardiac activity, common reference and residual artifacts can contribute. These four people establish neither pathway, causal propagation, a general participant population nor clinically useful prediction.

**Seal.** Fit endpoints, scalers, all-development weights, inference/parity fixtures, complete evaluation code and source identities before any outside test observation. The six assessment participants were scored separately after fitting. No model selection follows their outcomes in this study.
