# Source and reuse

Steven M. Peterson and Daniel P. Ferris released the source recordings under CC0. This forecast uses the visual-rotation walking condition, the recorded scalp and surface electrodes, and optical markers over the sacrum and head. The original authors describe the recording and their analyses here. [OpenNeuro snapshot 1.0.3](https://doi.org/10.18112/openneuro.ds003739.v1.0.3) · [Data in Brief, 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2021.107635) · [Original eNeuro study, 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6088363/)

The example is invented. The trained weights and aggregate errors derive from the recordings. NeuroTranslation dedicates its original code and materials in this package to the public domain to the extent of its rights. [CC0 1.0 Universal](LICENSE)
