NeuroTranslation and Brain2Body: the four papers

Two papers, each written twice: a Technical edition, and a Simple English edition that explains the same things in ordinary words. Start with a Simple English edition.

NeuroTranslation: A New Paradigm In Brain-Body Mapping.

What does a recording of the brain or of a muscle add to a forecast of a movement that already knows how the body has just been moving? The paper reports every study of the repository’s numbered table.

Brain2Body: The Next Generation In Body-Brain Interfacing.

What does a person keep when a machine guides their hand through a skill and then stops? The paper is a proposal: it reports no experiment on that question.
