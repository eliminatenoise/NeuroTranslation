NeuroTranslation and Brain2Body: the four papers

Two papers, each written twice: a Technical edition, and a Simple English edition that explains the same things in ordinary words. Start with a Simple English edition.

NeuroTranslation: A New Paradigm In Brain-Body Mapping.

NeuroTranslation tests how much brain and muscle recordings add to forecasts that already use recent movement. It reports all {{ n_in_papers() }} studies in the numbered table. Chapter 7 adds walking forecasts and a separate Next Neuron Prediction test on one rat recording.

Brain2Body: The Next Generation In Body-Brain Interfacing.

Brain2Body proposes testing what a person retains after a machine stops guiding their hand through a skill. The paper reports no experiment on skill retention.
