# Does yesterday’s calibration carry?

Press with the same finger on another day. In four previously scored Hyser participants, their own Day 1 readings gave a better Day 2 adjustment than a partner’s readings. The unchanged forecast had the smallest mean absolute error with either set of inputs. The papers compare that result with the earlier test fitted and scored on the same day.

The two Brain2Body editions also follow early anatomy, skin repair and milk across distinct marsupial species. Skeletal timing in dunnarts leaves the timing in Antechinus open. Skin scarring and changes in wallaby milk leave body-wide repair and precision developmental control open.
