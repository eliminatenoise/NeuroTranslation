R9 reading-aid metadata and packaging correction

R9 is the public working draft published at commit
a86bf81a308a71ccfa98bf2e0d5ec7ac329e9ba7. Its four manuscripts have matching
PDFs, and its terminology projection uses registry revision 34.

Some links in R9 name research-evidence.json from an older source workspace.
That JSON reading aid is absent from this public snapshot. The package still
has missing external dependencies and is not a standalone build. The older
local reading aid names registry revision 10 and describes the papers as
unpublished and awaiting compilation. Those fields do not describe the
compiled public R9 edition.

Use papers/current.json and the R9 manifest to identify the selected artifacts.
The older aid and its computations remain historical source evidence; they
have not been rerun or relabelled as new results. The previous version of this
note did not make the missing public file clear.

The complete source editorial audit requires a further revision. Public draft
availability establishes neither peer review nor completed scientific, rights,
reference, visual or export assessment. Current R9 visual inspection covers
72 of 520 pages; the other 448 pages remain uninspected. R10 source preparation
is underway and is not a selected or rendered replacement.

CC0 1.0 applies to project-owned contributions. Third-party material retains its
own terms and notices. The paper release does not establish a validated model
or GUI release.
