Some links in R9 open research-evidence.json, an older reading aid. That file
still names registry revision 10 and describes the papers as unpublished and
awaiting compilation. Those fields do not describe the compiled public R9
edition. Use papers/current.json and the R9 manifest to identify the selected
artifacts. The older reading aid is retained as historical evidence; its
computations have not been rerun or relabelled as new results.

The complete source editorial audit requires a further revision. Public draft
availability establishes neither peer review nor completed scientific, rights,
reference, visual or export assessment. Current R9 visual inspection covers
72 of 520 pages; the other 448 pages remain uninspected. R10 source preparation
is underway and is not a selected or rendered replacement.
